📘 Chapter 9 – Gravitation
Q1. What is gravitation?

Answer: Gravitation is the force of attraction between any two objects having mass.

Q2. State the universal law of gravitation.

Answer: Every object in the universe attracts every other object with a force which is directly proportional to the product of their masses and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between their centres.

F = Gm₁m₂ / r²

where G is the universal gravitational constant.

Q3. What is the universal gravitational constant?

Answer: The universal gravitational constant is denoted by G. Its SI value is approximately:

G = 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m²/kg²

Q4. What is free fall?

Answer: The motion of an object under the influence of gravitational force alone is called free fall.

Q5. What is acceleration due to gravity?

Answer: The acceleration produced in a freely falling object due to the gravitational force of the Earth is called acceleration due to gravity. It is represented by g.

Near the surface of Earth:

g ≈ 9.8 m/s²

Q6. Why is the value of acceleration due to gravity different at different places on Earth?

Answer: The Earth is not a perfect sphere and its rotation also affects gravitational acceleration. Therefore, the value of g varies slightly from place to place.

Q7. What is the difference between mass and weight?
Mass Weight
Mass is the amount of matter in an object. Weight is the gravitational force acting on an object.
Its SI unit is kilogram (kg). Its SI unit is newton (N).
Mass remains approximately constant at different places. Weight can change from place to place.

Weight = mg

Q8. Why does an object weigh less on the Moon than on the Earth?

Answer: The Moon has much less mass than the Earth, so its gravitational pull is weaker. The acceleration due to gravity on the Moon is about one-sixth of that on Earth. Therefore, an object weighs less on the Moon.

Q9. What is thrust?

Answer: The force acting normally on a surface is called thrust.

Q10. What is pressure?

Answer: Pressure is the force acting normally on a unit area of a surface.

Pressure = Force / Area

The SI unit of pressure is pascal (Pa).

Q11. Why does a sharp knife cut better than a blunt knife?

Answer: A sharp knife has a smaller area of contact. For the same force, it produces greater pressure and therefore cuts more easily.

Q12. Why are school bags provided with broad straps?

Answer: Broad straps increase the area over which the weight of the bag acts. Therefore, the pressure on the shoulders decreases and the bag is more comfortable to carry.

Q13. State Archimedes' principle.

Answer: When an object is wholly or partially immersed in a fluid, it experiences an upward force equal to the weight of the fluid displaced by it.

Q14. What is buoyancy?

Answer: The upward force exerted by a fluid on an object immersed in it is called buoyant force or buoyancy.

Q15. What factors affect buoyant force?

Answer: Buoyant force depends mainly on:

  1. The density of the fluid.
  2. The volume of fluid displaced by the object.
  3. The acceleration due to gravity.
Q16. Why does an object float or sink in water?

Answer: An object floats when the buoyant force is equal to or balances its weight. An object sinks when its weight is greater than the buoyant force.

Q17. Why does a ship made of iron float on water?

Answer: A ship is hollow and contains a large volume of air. Its average density becomes less than that of water, so it displaces enough water to produce an upward buoyant force that supports its weight.

Q18. What is density?

Answer: Density is the mass per unit volume of a substance.

Density = Mass / Volume

The SI unit of density is kg/m³.

Q19. What is relative density?

Answer: Relative density is the ratio of the density of a substance to the density of water at a specified reference condition.

Relative density = Density of substance / Density of water

Relative density has no unit.

Q20. Why does a stone released from a height fall towards the Earth?

Answer: The Earth attracts the stone towards its centre due to gravitational force. Therefore, the stone falls towards the Earth.

Q21. What happens to the gravitational force between two objects if the distance between them is doubled?

Answer: According to the universal law of gravitation:

F ∝ 1/r²

If the distance is doubled, the gravitational force becomes one-fourth of its original value.

Q22. What happens to the gravitational force if the mass of one object is doubled?

Answer: Since gravitational force is directly proportional to the product of the masses, doubling one mass doubles the gravitational force.

Q23. Why do all objects fall towards the Earth with the same acceleration in the absence of air resistance?

Answer: The acceleration due to gravity is independent of the mass of the falling object. Therefore, in the absence of air resistance, all objects fall with the same acceleration g.

🧮 Important Numericals
Q24. Calculate the weight of an object of mass 10 kg on Earth.

Solution:

Mass, m = 10 kg

Acceleration due to gravity, g = 9.8 m/s²

Weight = mg

W = 10 × 9.8 = 98 N

Therefore, the weight of the object is 98 N.

Q25. Calculate the pressure when a force of 100 N acts on an area of 2 m².

Solution:

Force, F = 100 N

Area, A = 2 m²

Pressure = F/A

P = 100/2 = 50 Pa

Therefore, the pressure is 50 Pa.

Q26. An object has a mass of 500 g and a volume of 250 cm³. Find its density.

Solution:

Mass = 500 g

Volume = 250 cm³

Density = Mass / Volume

Density = 500/250 = 2 g/cm³

Therefore, the density is 2 g/cm³.
