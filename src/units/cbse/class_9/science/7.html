📘 Chapter 7 – Motion
Q1. What is motion?

Answer: Motion is the change in position of an object with respect to time and a reference point.

Q2. What is meant by rest?

Answer: An object is said to be at rest when its position does not change with respect to a reference point with time.

Q3. What is a reference point?

Answer: A fixed point with respect to which the position of an object is described is called a reference point.

Q4. Define distance.

Answer: Distance is the total length of the actual path travelled by an object during its motion.

SI unit: metre (m)

Q5. Define displacement.

Answer: Displacement is the shortest distance between the initial and final positions of an object in a particular direction.

SI unit: metre (m)

Q6. Write two differences between distance and displacement.
Distance Displacement
It is the actual path travelled by an object. It is the shortest distance between initial and final positions.
It is a scalar quantity. It is a vector quantity.
It is always positive. It may be positive, negative or zero.
Q7. What is speed?

Answer: Speed is the distance travelled by an object per unit time.

Formula:
Speed = Distance / Time

SI unit: metre per second (m/s)

Q8. What is average speed?

Answer: Average speed is the total distance travelled divided by the total time taken.

Formula:
Average Speed = Total Distance / Total Time

Q9. What is velocity?

Answer: Velocity is the displacement of an object per unit time.

Formula:
Velocity = Displacement / Time

SI unit: metre per second (m/s)

Q10. What is average velocity?

Answer: Average velocity is the total displacement divided by the total time taken.

Formula:
Average Velocity = Total Displacement / Total Time

Q11. What is uniform motion?

Answer: When an object covers equal distances in equal intervals of time, it is said to be in uniform motion.

Q12. What is non-uniform motion?

Answer: When an object covers unequal distances in equal intervals of time or equal distances in unequal intervals of time, it is said to be in non-uniform motion.

Q13. What is acceleration?

Answer: Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity with time.

Formula:
a = (v - u) / t

where u is initial velocity, v is final velocity and t is time.

SI unit: m/s²

Q14. What is uniform acceleration?

Answer: When the velocity of an object changes by equal amounts in equal intervals of time, the acceleration is called uniform acceleration.

Q15. What is non-uniform acceleration?

Answer: When the velocity of an object changes by unequal amounts in equal intervals of time, the acceleration is called non-uniform acceleration.

Q16. What is retardation?

Answer: Retardation is the negative acceleration or the rate at which the velocity of an object decreases with time.

Q17. What is a scalar quantity?

Answer: A physical quantity which has magnitude only and no direction is called a scalar quantity.

Examples: Distance, speed and time.

Q18. What is a vector quantity?

Answer: A physical quantity which has both magnitude and direction is called a vector quantity.

Examples: Displacement, velocity and acceleration.

Q19. Write the three equations of motion.

Answer: The three equations of motion for uniformly accelerated motion are:

First equation: v = u + at

Second equation: s = ut + ½at²

Third equation: v² - u² = 2as

Here, u = initial velocity, v = final velocity, a = acceleration, t = time and s = displacement.

Q20. What does the slope of a distance-time graph represent?

Answer: The slope of a distance-time graph represents the speed of the object.

Speed = Change in distance / Change in time

Q21. What does the slope of a velocity-time graph represent?

Answer: The slope of a velocity-time graph represents the acceleration of the object.

Acceleration = Change in velocity / Time

Q22. What does the area under a velocity-time graph represent?

Answer: The area under a velocity-time graph represents the displacement of the object.

Q23. What is the difference between speed and velocity?
Speed Velocity
Speed is distance travelled per unit time. Velocity is displacement per unit time.
It is a scalar quantity. It is a vector quantity.
It cannot be negative. It can be positive, negative or zero.
Q24. Can an object have zero displacement but non-zero distance?

Answer: Yes. If an object completes a round trip and returns to its initial position, its displacement is zero but the distance travelled is not zero.

Q25. Can the magnitude of displacement be greater than distance?

Answer: No. The magnitude of displacement is always less than or equal to the distance travelled.

Q26. When is the magnitude of displacement equal to distance?

Answer: The magnitude of displacement is equal to distance when an object moves along a straight line in one direction without changing its direction.

Q27. A car travels 100 m in 5 seconds. Calculate its speed.

Given:
Distance = 100 m
Time = 5 s

Speed = Distance / Time

Speed = 100 / 5 = 20 m/s

Answer: The speed of the car is 20 m/s.

Q28. A car starts from rest and accelerates uniformly at 2 m/s² for 5 s. Find its final velocity.

Given:
u = 0 m/s
a = 2 m/s²
t = 5 s

Using the equation:

v = u + at

v = 0 + (2 × 5)

v = 10 m/s

Answer: The final velocity is 10 m/s.

Q29. A car starts from rest and accelerates at 2 m/s² for 5 seconds. Find the distance travelled.

Given:
u = 0 m/s
a = 2 m/s²
t = 5 s

Using:

s = ut + ½at²

s = 0 + ½ × 2 × 5²

s = 25 m

Answer: The distance travelled is 25 m.

Q30. What is the SI unit of speed, velocity and acceleration?

Speed: metre per second (m/s)

Velocity: metre per second (m/s)

Acceleration: metre per second squared (m/s²)
