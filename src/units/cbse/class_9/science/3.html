📘 Chapter 3 – Atoms and Molecules
Q1. What is an atom?

Answer: An atom is the smallest particle of an element that retains all the chemical properties of that element and takes part in chemical reactions.

Q2. What is a molecule?

Answer: A molecule is the smallest particle of an element or compound that can exist independently and shows all the properties of that substance.

Q3. State the law of conservation of mass.

Answer: The law of conservation of mass states that mass can neither be created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction. The total mass of the reactants is equal to the total mass of the products.

Q4. State the law of constant proportions.

Answer: The law of constant proportions states that a pure chemical compound always contains the same elements combined together in the same fixed proportion by mass.

For example, water always contains hydrogen and oxygen in the mass ratio 1 : 8.

Q5. State the main postulates of Dalton's atomic theory.

Answer: The main postulates of Dalton's atomic theory are:

  1. All matter is made up of very small particles called atoms.
  2. Atoms of the same element have the same mass and chemical properties.
  3. Atoms of different elements have different masses and chemical properties.
  4. Atoms cannot be created or destroyed in a chemical reaction.
  5. Atoms combine in simple whole-number ratios to form compounds.
  6. The number and kind of atoms remain unchanged in a chemical reaction.

Q7. What is atomic mass?

Answer: Atomic mass is the mass of an atom expressed in atomic mass unit (u). One atomic mass unit is defined as one-twelfth of the mass of one carbon-12 atom.

Q8. What is molecular mass?

Answer: Molecular mass is the sum of the atomic masses of all the atoms present in one molecule of a substance. It is expressed in atomic mass unit (u).

Example: Molecular mass of H2O

= 2 × 1 + 16 = 18 u

Q9. What is formula unit mass?

Answer: Formula unit mass is the sum of the atomic masses of all the atoms represented in the formula unit of an ionic compound.

Example: Formula unit mass of NaCl

= 23 + 35.5 = 58.5 u

Q10. What is a mole?

Answer: A mole is the amount of substance that contains 6.022 × 1023 particles. This number is called Avogadro's number.

1 mole = 6.022 × 1023 particles

Q11. What is Avogadro's number?

Answer: Avogadro's number is the number of particles present in one mole of a substance.

NA = 6.022 × 1023 particles per mole.

Q12. What is the difference between an atom and a molecule?
Atom Molecule
An atom is the smallest particle of an element. A molecule is the smallest independently existing particle of an element or compound.
An atom may or may not exist independently. A molecule can exist independently.
Example: H, O, Na Example: H2, O2, H2O
Q13. What is meant by valency?

Answer: Valency is the combining capacity of an atom of an element. It depends on the number of electrons lost, gained or shared by an atom during chemical combination.

For example, the valency of sodium is 1, magnesium is 2 and aluminium is 3.

Q14. Write the chemical formulae of the following compounds: magnesium chloride, calcium oxide and aluminium oxide.

Answer:

  • Magnesium chloride – MgCl2
  • Calcium oxide – CaO
  • Aluminium oxide – Al2O3
Q15. Write the chemical formulae of sodium sulphide and calcium chloride.

Answer:

  • Sodium sulphide – Na2S
  • Calcium chloride – CaCl2
Q16. Calculate the molecular mass of H2O.

Answer:

Atomic mass of H = 1 u

Atomic mass of O = 16 u

Molecular mass of H2O = 2 × 1 + 16 = 18 u

Q17. Calculate the molecular mass of CO2.

Answer:

Atomic mass of C = 12 u

Atomic mass of O = 16 u

Molecular mass of CO2 = 12 + (2 × 16) = 44 u

Q18. Calculate the formula unit mass of CaCl2.

Answer:

Atomic mass of Ca = 40 u

Atomic mass of Cl = 35.5 u

Formula unit mass of CaCl2 = 40 + (2 × 35.5) = 111 u

Q19. Calculate the number of molecules present in 18 g of water.

Answer:

Molar mass of water = 18 g/mol

Therefore, 18 g of water = 1 mole of water.

Number of molecules = 6.022 × 1023

Answer = 6.022 × 1023 molecules

Q20. How many atoms are present in one mole of sodium?

Answer: One mole of sodium contains Avogadro's number of atoms.

Therefore,

Number of sodium atoms = 6.022 × 1023 atoms.

Q21. What is the difference between 2H and H2?

Answer:

  • 2H represents two separate hydrogen atoms.
  • H2 represents one molecule of hydrogen containing two hydrogen atoms.
Q22. What is the difference between 2O and O2?

Answer:

  • 2O represents two separate oxygen atoms.
  • O2 represents one oxygen molecule containing two oxygen atoms.
Q23. Why cannot an atom be divided during a chemical reaction according to Dalton's theory?

Answer: According to Dalton's atomic theory, an atom is the smallest particle of an element that takes part in a chemical reaction. During a chemical reaction, atoms are only rearranged; they are neither created nor destroyed.

Q24. Why do atoms combine to form molecules?

Answer: Atoms combine with one another to attain a stable electronic configuration. They form molecules by sharing, gaining or losing electrons.
