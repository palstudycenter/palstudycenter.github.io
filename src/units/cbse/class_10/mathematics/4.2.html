Question 1 (i): Find the roots of the quadratic equation \(x^2-3x-10=0\) by factorisation.

Solution -

Given equation:

\[ x^2-3x-10=0 \]

Factorising,

\[ x^2-5x+2x-10=0 \]

\[ x(x-5)+2(x-5)=0 \]

\[ (x-5)(x+2)=0 \]

Therefore,

\[ x-5=0 \quad \text{or} \quad x+2=0 \]

Hence,

\[ \boxed{x=5,\;x=-2} \]

Question 1 (ii): Find the roots of the quadratic equation \(2x^2+x-6=0\) by factorisation.

Solution -

Given equation:

\[ 2x^2+x-6=0 \]

Factorising,

\[ 2x^2+4x-3x-6=0 \]

\[ 2x(x+2)-3(x+2)=0 \]

\[ (x+2)(2x-3)=0 \]

Therefore,

\[ x+2=0 \quad \text{or} \quad 2x-3=0 \]

Hence,

\[ \boxed{x=-2,\;x=\frac32} \]

Question 1 (iii): Find the roots of the quadratic equation \(\sqrt2x^2+7x+5\sqrt2=0\) by factorisation.

Solution -

Given equation:

\[ \sqrt2x^2+7x+5\sqrt2=0 \]

Factorising,

\[ (\sqrt2x+5)(x+\sqrt2)=0 \]

Therefore,

\[ \sqrt2x+5=0 \quad\text{or}\quad x+\sqrt2=0 \]

Hence,

\[ \boxed{x=-\frac{5}{\sqrt2},\;x=-\sqrt2} \]

Question 1 (iv): Find the roots of the quadratic equation \(2x^2-x+\frac18=0\) by factorisation.

Solution -

Given equation:

\[ 2x^2-x+\frac18=0 \]

Multiplying throughout by 8,

\[ 16x^2-8x+1=0 \]

Factorising,

\[ (4x-1)^2=0 \]

Therefore,

\[ 4x-1=0 \]

Hence,

\[ \boxed{x=\frac14,\;\frac14} \]

Question 2: Solve the problems given in Example 1.

Solution -

Example 1 asks us to find two numbers whose difference is 3 and whose product is 504.

Let the smaller number be \(x\).

Then the larger number is \((x+3)\).

According to the question,

\[ x(x+3)=504 \]

Bringing all the terms to one side,

\[ x^2+3x-504=0 \]

Factorising,

\[ (x+24)(x-21)=0 \]

Therefore,

\[ x=-24 \quad \text{or} \quad x=21 \]

Since the numbers are positive,

The smaller number is 21 and the larger number is 24.

Question 3: Find two numbers whose sum is 27 and product is 182.

Solution -

Let one number be \(x\).

Then the other number is \((27-x)\).

According to the question,

\[ x(27-x)=182 \]

Simplifying,

\[ 27x-x^2=182 \]

\[ x^2-27x+182=0 \]

Factorising,

\[ (x-13)(x-14)=0 \]

Therefore,

\[ x=13 \quad \text{or} \quad x=14 \]

Hence, the required numbers are

13 and 14.

Question 4: Find two consecutive positive integers, sum of whose squares is 365.

Solution -

Let the first positive integer be \(x\).

Then the next consecutive integer is \((x+1)\).

According to the question,

\[ x^2+(x+1)^2=365 \]

Simplifying,

\[ 2x^2+2x+1=365 \]

\[ 2x^2+2x-364=0 \]

\[ x^2+x-182=0 \]

Factorising,

\[ (x+14)(x-13)=0 \]

Therefore,

\[ x=-14 \quad \text{or} \quad x=13 \]

Since the integers are positive,

The required integers are 13 and 14.

Question 5: The altitude of a right triangle is 7 cm less than its base. If the hypotenuse is 13 cm, find the other two sides.

Solution -

Let the base of the right triangle be \(x\) cm.

Then, the altitude is \((x-7)\) cm.

Hypotenuse = 13 cm

Using Pythagoras theorem,

\[ x^2+(x-7)^2=13^2 \]

Simplifying,

\[ x^2+x^2-14x+49=169 \]

\[ 2x^2-14x-120=0 \]

\[ x^2-7x-60=0 \]

Factorising,

\[ (x-12)(x+5)=0 \]

Therefore,

\[ x=12 \quad \text{or} \quad x=-5 \]

Since length cannot be negative,

Base = 12 cm

Altitude = 12 − 7 = 5 cm

The other two sides of the triangle are 12 cm and 5 cm.

Question 6: A cottage industry produces a certain number of pottery articles in a day. It was observed on a particular day that the cost of production of each article (in rupees) was 3 more than twice the number of articles produced on that day. If the total cost of production on that day was 90, find the number of articles produced and the cost of each article.

Solution -

Let the number of pottery articles produced in a day be \(x\).

Then, the cost of production of each article is \((2x+3)\) rupees.

According to the question,

\[ x(2x+3)=90 \]

Bringing all the terms to one side,

\[ 2x^2+3x-90=0 \]

Factorising,

\[ 2x^2+15x-12x-90=0 \]

\[ x(2x+15)-6(2x+15)=0 \]

\[ (2x+15)(x-6)=0 \]

Therefore,

\[ 2x+15=0 \quad \text{or} \quad x-6=0 \]

\[ x=-\frac{15}{2} \quad \text{or} \quad x=6 \]

Since the number of articles cannot be negative,

Number of articles produced = 6

Cost of production of each article

\[ =2(6)+3=15 \]

Therefore,

Number of articles produced = 6

Cost of each article = ₹15
