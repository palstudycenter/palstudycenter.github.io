Question 1: Find the zeroes of the following polynomial and verify the relationship between the zeroes and the coefficients.

(i) x2 – 2x – 8

Answer:

Given polynomial:

p(x) = x2 – 2x – 8

p(x) = (x – 4)(x + 2)

For zeroes, p(x) = 0

(x – 4)(x + 2) = 0

Therefore, the zeroes are:

x = 4 and x = –2

Verification:

Here, a = 1, b = –2, c = –8

Sum of zeroes = 4 + (–2) = 2

–b/a = –(–2)/1 = 2

Hence, Sum of zeroes = –b/a ✔


Product of zeroes = 4 × (–2) = –8

c/a = –8/1 = –8

Hence, Product of zeroes = c/a ✔

Therefore, the relationship between the zeroes and the coefficients is verified.

Question 1 (ii): 4s2 – 4s + 1

Answer:

p(s) = 4s2 – 4s + 1

p(s) = (2s – 1)(2s – 1)

For zeroes, p(s) = 0

(2s – 1)(2s – 1) = 0

Therefore, the zeroes are:

s = 1/2 and s = 1/2

Verification:

Here, a = 4, b = –4, c = 1

Sum of zeroes = 1/2 + 1/2 = 1

–b/a = –(–4)/4 = 1

Hence, Sum of zeroes = –b/a ✔


Product of zeroes = (1/2) × (1/2) = 1/4

c/a = 1/4

Hence, Product of zeroes = c/a ✔

Therefore, the relationship between the zeroes and the coefficients is verified.

c/a = –3/6 = –1/2

Hence, Product of zeroes = c/a ✔

Therefore, the relationship between the zeroes and the coefficients is verified.

Question 2 (i): Find a quadratic polynomial whose sum of zeroes is \(\frac{1}{4}\) and product of zeroes is \(-1\).

Answer:

Given

Sum of zeroes (\(\alpha+\beta\)) = \(\frac{1}{4}\)

Product of zeroes (\(\alpha\beta\)) = \(-1\)

The quadratic polynomial whose zeroes are \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\) is:

\[ P(x)=x^2-(\alpha+\beta)x+\alpha\beta \]

\[ P(x)=x^2-\frac14x-1 \]

\[ P(x)=4x^2-x-4 \]
