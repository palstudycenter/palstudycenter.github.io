📚 Unit 7 – Lifelines of National Economy
Q1. What are called the lifelines of the national economy?
Answer: The means of transport and communication are called the lifelines of the national economy, because through them different parts of the country remain connected with one another.
Q2. What is transport?
Answer: The process of carrying people and goods from one place to another is called transport.
Q3. What are the main means of transport?
Answer: The main means of transport are roadways, railways, pipelines, waterways and airways.
Q4. What is the importance of road transport in India?
Answer: Road transport connects villages, towns and cities. It is extremely useful for short-distance travel and for carrying goods.
Q5. Into how many major categories are roads divided?
Answer: In India, roads are mainly divided into categories such as national highways, state highways, district roads, rural roads and border roads.
Q6. What are national highways?
Answer: Roads that connect the country's major cities, state capitals, ports and important industrial areas are called national highways.
Q7. What are state highways?
Answer: Roads that connect the state capital with district headquarters and other important towns of the state are called state highways.
Q8. What are district roads?
Answer: Roads that connect district headquarters with other important places in the district are called district roads.
Q9. What are rural roads?
Answer: Roads that connect villages with nearby villages, towns and markets are called rural roads.
Q10. What are border roads?
Answer: Roads that connect strategically important areas near the country's borders are called border roads.
Q11. What is the Golden Quadrilateral project?
Answer: Under the Golden Quadrilateral project, Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai and Kolkata have been connected by four-lane roads.
Q12. What are the North-South and East-West Corridors?
Answer: The North-South Corridor connects important places from Srinagar to Kanyakumari, while the East-West Corridor connects important places from Silchar to Porbandar.
Q13. What is the importance of railway transport?
Answer: Railway transport is the main means of carrying passengers and large quantities of goods over long distances at low cost.
Q14. What are the factors affecting the development of railway transport in India?
Answer: Topography, economic development, distribution of population and distribution of resources affect the development of railway transport.
Q15. Where is the network of railways denser in India?
Answer: In India, the network of railways is denser in the northern plains and industrial areas.
Q16. What is pipeline transport?
Answer: Carrying petroleum, natural gas, water and other liquid materials from one place to another through pipes is called pipeline transport.
Q17. Write two advantages of pipeline transport.
Answer: (1) The cost of transport is low. (2) It provides continuous and safe means of transport.
Q18. Which goods are transported through pipelines in India?
Answer: Crude oil, petroleum products, natural gas and water are transported through pipelines.
Q19. What is water transport?
Answer: Carrying passengers and goods from one place to another through rivers, canals, lakes and seas is called water transport.
Q20. What are inland waterways?
Answer: Transport carried out through inland water sources such as rivers, canals and lakes is called inland water transport.
Q21. What are the major inland waterways of India?
Answer: The Ganga-Bhagirathi-Hugli river system, the Brahmaputra river and the backwaters of Kerala are included among the major inland waterways of India.
Q22. Why is water transport called cheap transport?
Answer: In water transport, fuel consumption is relatively low, and heavy goods can be carried over long distances at low cost.
Q23. Write the names of India's major seaports.
Answer: Mumbai, Jawaharlal Nehru Port, Kandla, Kochi, Chennai, Visakhapatnam, Paradip and Kolkata are the major ports.
Q24. What is air transport?
Answer: Carrying passengers and goods from one place to another by aircraft is called air transport.
Q25. What is the main advantage of airways?
Answer: Airways are the fastest means of transport. They are especially useful for connecting inaccessible and remote areas.
Q26. Write one major drawback of air transport.
Answer: Air transport is more expensive than other means and can be affected by bad weather.
Q27. What is communication?
Answer: The process of conveying ideas, information and messages from one person or place to another is called communication.
Q28. What are the main means of communication?
Answer: Postal service, telephone, mobile, internet, radio, television, newspapers and satellite communication are the main means.
Q29. What is mass communication?
Answer: When a message or information is conveyed to a large number of people at the same time, it is called mass communication.
Q30. What are the main means of mass communication?
Answer: Radio, television, newspapers, internet and cinema are the main means of mass communication.
Q31. What is international trade?
Answer: The exchange of goods and services between two or more countries is called international trade.
Q32. What is the difference between import and export?
Answer: Buying goods from foreign countries is called import, while selling goods made in the country to foreign countries is called export.
Q33. What is the balance of trade?
Answer: The difference between the value of a country's exports and imports is called the balance of trade.
Q34. What is a favourable balance of trade?
Answer: When a country's exports are more than its imports, it is called a favourable balance of trade.
Q35. What is an unfavourable balance of trade?
Answer: When a country's imports are more than its exports, it is called an unfavourable balance of trade.
Q36. What is tourism?
Answer: Travelling from one place to another for the purpose of recreation, education, religion, culture or other purposes is called tourism.
Q37. What is the economic importance of tourism?
Answer: Tourism brings in foreign exchange, increases employment, promotes local industries and increases the country's income.
Q38. What are the factors that promote tourism?
Answer: Natural beauty, historical sites, religious sites, cultural diversity, wildlife and better transport facilities promote tourism.
Q39. What is the importance of tourism in India?
Answer: Tourism provides employment, earns foreign exchange, and gives India's culture and natural heritage recognition at the global level.
Q40. Why are transport and communication called the lifelines of the economy?
Answer: Transport and communication connect different regions, markets, industries and people of the country. Through them, the exchange of goods, people and information happens easily. That is why they are called the lifelines of the economy.
