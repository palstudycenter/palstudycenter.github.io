5-Consumer Rights
Question 1. Who is called a consumer?
Answer: A person who buys goods or services for his own use and consumes them is called a consumer.
Question 2. What is consumer awareness?
Answer: Having knowledge about one's rights, the quality and price of goods, and the legal protection available is called consumer awareness.
Question 3. Why is consumer protection necessary?
Answer: Consumer protection is necessary to safeguard consumers from problems such as adulteration, incorrect weights and measures, poor quality, overpricing and misleading advertisements.
Question 4. What are the main rights of a consumer?
Answer: The right to safety, the right to information, the right to choose, the right to be heard, the right to seek redressal and the right to consumer education are the main rights.
Question 5. What is the right to safety?
Answer: A consumer has the right to be protected against goods and services that may be hazardous to his life and property.
Question 6. What is the right to information?
Answer: A consumer has the right to obtain information about the quality, quantity, price, purity and other necessary details of a good or service.
Question 7. What is the right to choose?
Answer: A consumer has the right to choose from a variety of goods and services at a fair price, according to his own preference.
Question 8. What is the right to seek redressal?
Answer: The right to have complaints arising from unfair trade practices or exploitation resolved, and to receive fair compensation, is called the right to seek redressal.
Question 9. What is the importance of consumer education?
Answer: Consumer education informs people about their rights and responsibilities and helps them make correct decisions.
Question 10. What does the ISI mark indicate?
Answer: The ISI mark generally indicates that an industrial product conforms to the prescribed quality standards.
Question 11. What is Hallmark?
Answer: Hallmark is a certification mark related to the purity of precious metal jewellery such as gold and silver.
Question 12. What things should a consumer keep in mind while making a purchase?
Answer: A consumer must check necessary information such as the MRP, quantity, quality mark, manufacturing and expiry dates, warranty, and the bill.
Question 13. Why does a consumer file a complaint?
Answer: A consumer can file a complaint in case of a defective product, faulty service, incorrect information, unfair pricing or any other unfair trade practice.
Question 14. What is the role of the government in protecting consumer rights?
Answer: The government makes laws, sets quality standards, provides support to consumer organisations, and makes arrangements for the redressal of complaints.
Question 15. Write two characteristics of an aware consumer.
Answer: (1) He checks the quality and price while buying a product. (2) He keeps the bill and necessary proof safe and remains aware of his rights.
