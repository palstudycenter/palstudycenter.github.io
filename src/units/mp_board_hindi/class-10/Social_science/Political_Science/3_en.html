📚 3 - Caste, Religion and Gender Issues
Question 1. What is gender division?
Answer: The separate assignment of tasks and roles to women and men in society is called gender division.
Question 2. What is the sexual division of labour?
Answer: When work is divided on the basis of gender, this is called the sexual division of labour.
Question 3. What is gender inequality?
Answer: The absence of equality between women and men in rights, opportunities, education, employment and wages is called gender inequality.
Question 4. What is patriarchy?
Answer: A social system in which men are given more rights and importance than women in the family and society is called patriarchy.
Question 5. Who is a feminist?
Answer: A person who supports equal rights and opportunities for women and men is called a feminist.
Question 6. What is the feminist movement?
Answer: The movement carried out to secure equal rights, opportunities and respect for women, equal to men, is called the feminist movement.
Question 7. What were the main demands of the feminist movement? / What are the political demands of the feminist movement in India?
Answer: The demand for equal rights, education, employment, equal wages and political representation for women were the main demands of the feminist movement.
Question 8. What is the Equal Wages Act?
Answer: According to the Equal Wages Act, women and men should receive equal wages for the same kind of work.
Question 9. What is the status of women's representation in legislatures in India?
Answer: The representation of women in India's legislatures has remained lower than that of men for a long time. Reservation for women in local bodies has increased their political participation.
Question 10. What has been done to increase women's political participation in India?
Answer: At least one-third of the seats in institutions of local self-government were reserved for women. This increased women's political participation.
Question 11. What is communalism?
Answer: When religion is made the basis of politics and the interests of one religious community are considered separate from or opposed to another community, this is called communalism.
Question 12. What is secularism?
Answer: Secularism means that the state gives equal respect to all religions and does not give special priority to any one religion as the state religion.
Question 13. What is a secular state?
Answer: A state that does not treat any one religion as its official religion and gives equal rights to people of all religions is called a secular state.
Question 14. State any two constitutional provisions that make India a secular state.
Answer: (1) The Constitution gives all citizens religious freedom. (2) Discrimination on the basis of religion, caste, etc. is prohibited.
Question 15. What is family law?
Answer: Laws relating to family matters such as marriage, divorce, adoption, inheritance, etc. are called family law.
Question 16. What is the caste system?
Answer: A social system in which society is divided into different castes and groups on the basis of birth is called the caste system.
Question 17. What is caste hierarchy?
Answer: The system of ranking castes in an order of high and low is called caste hierarchy.
Question 18. What is casteism?
Answer: When a person considers their own caste superior to other castes and gives priority to its interests, this is called casteism.
Question 19. What is a vote bank?
Answer: A group of voters that consistently supports a particular political party or candidate is called a vote bank.
Question 20. What is urbanisation?
Answer: When people move from villages to cities in search of employment, education and other opportunities, and cities expand, this is called urbanisation.
Question 21. Name four social reformers who spoke of building a society free from caste discrimination.
Answer: Jyotiba Phule, Dr. Bhimrao Ambedkar, Mahatma Gandhi and Shri Narayana Guru.
Question 22. What does the Indian Constitution say about caste discrimination?
Answer: The Constitution prohibits discrimination on the basis of caste and abolishes untouchability.
Question 23. What effect does caste have on politics?
Answer: Caste can influence the selection of candidates in elections, the support of voters, and the policies of political parties.
Question 24. Give two reasons why caste alone cannot determine the outcome of elections in India.
Answer: (1) Not all people of a caste vote for the same party. (2) A candidate's popularity, a party's policies and other socio-economic issues are also important in an election.
Question 25. Explain how social inequality is an obstacle to the country's development.
Answer: Because of social inequality, not everyone gets equal opportunities in education, employment and resources. As a result, human resources are not fully utilised, and the country's development is affected.
Question 26. What measures have been taken to reduce social inequality?
Answer: Efforts to reduce social inequality have been made through the constitutional right to equality, the abolition of untouchability, reservation, educational opportunities, and social welfare schemes.
Question 27. What is meant by adult franchise?
Answer: Adult franchise means that every citizen who has reached the prescribed age gets the right to vote, without discrimination on the basis of caste, religion, gender or economic status.
