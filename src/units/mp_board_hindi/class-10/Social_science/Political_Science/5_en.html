📚 5-Outcomes of Democracy
Q1. What is democracy? What is meant by democracy?
Answer: Democracy is a form of government in which the rulers are chosen by the people and the government is accountable to the people.
Q2. What is the main feature of democracy?
Answer: The main feature of democracy is that the final decision-making power rests with the representatives elected by the people.
Q3. What is meant by dictatorship?
Answer: A form of government in which power is concentrated in the hands of one person or a small group, and the people get limited participation in governance, is called dictatorship.
Q4. What is majority rule?
Answer: When priority is given to the votes or wishes of the majority in decision-making, it is called majority rule. In a democracy, along with the majority, respect for the rights of minorities is also necessary.
Q5. What is the dilemma regarding democracy?
Answer: Democracy is generally considered a better form of government, but it does not solve all problems immediately. Decision-making may take time, and social and economic inequalities may persist. This is the dilemma about democracy.
Q6. How does democracy create accountable government?
Answer: In a democracy, the government is elected by the people and must remain accountable to the people for its actions. Elections, the opposition, the media, and public opinion make the government accountable.
Q7. How does democracy produce an accountable, responsive, and legitimate government?
Answer: In a democracy, people elect their representatives. The government works according to the needs of the people and remains accountable to the people for its decisions. Free and fair elections give legitimacy to the government.
Q8. What is legitimate government?
Answer: A government that has the acceptance of the people and works according to the constitution and law is called a legitimate government.
Q9. Why is a democratic government legitimate?
Answer: A democratic government is elected by the people through free and fair elections. Therefore, it gets the acceptance of the people and the legitimate right to govern.
Q10. Why is a democratic form of government better than other forms of government?
Answer: Democracy provides an accountable and responsive government, improves the quality of decisions, resolves differences peacefully, and promotes the dignity and freedom of citizens.
Q11. Democracy is a better form of government. Explain.
Answer: In a democracy, people get the right to choose the government. It creates an accountable, responsive, and legitimate government and provides citizens with equality, freedom, and dignity. Therefore, democracy is considered a better system of governance.
Q12. Why is there a delay in taking important decisions in a democracy?
Answer: In a democracy, before taking a decision, consultation is done with various institutions, representatives, and interest groups. This process takes time, so there may be a delay in decision-making.
Q13. How does a democratic government provide transparent governance?
Answer: In a democracy, the government has to provide information about its decisions and actions to the people. The right to information, a free media, and public debate increase transparency in the government.
Q14. How is a democratic government transparent?
Answer: A democratic government places information about its decisions, policies, and actions before the people. Citizens have the right to obtain information about government activities.
Q15. What is the Right to Information?
Answer: The Right to Information gives citizens the legal right to obtain information related to the work, decisions, and records of public authorities.
Q16. What are the sources for obtaining information about the government?
Answer: Information about the government can be obtained from government websites, government reports, proceedings of Parliament and state legislatures, the Right to Information, newspapers, and other reliable sources.
Q17. Why are free and fair elections necessary for democracy?
Answer: Free and fair elections give citizens the opportunity to choose representatives of their choice. This gives the government the acceptance of the people and strengthens the democratic system.
Q18. Why can the quality of decisions be better in a democracy?
Answer: In a democracy, before a decision is taken, discussion, deliberation, and the opinions of various people are considered. This reduces the chances of mistakes and allows better decisions to be made.
Q19. What factors does economic development depend on?
Answer: Economic development depends on many factors such as natural resources, human resources, capital, technology, education, health, infrastructure, and government policies.
Q20. What are the factors that determine the economic development of a country?
Answer: The economic development of a country is influenced by per capita income, education, health, employment, industrialisation, agriculture, technology, infrastructure facilities, and natural and human resources.
Q21. What are the outcomes of democracy?
Answer: The main outcomes of democracy are — accountable and responsive government, better decisions, peaceful resolution of differences, dignity and freedom of citizens, and accommodation of social diversities.
Q22. What is the most important outcome of democracy?
Answer: The most important outcome of democracy is the creation of a government that is accountable, responsive, and legitimate to the people.
Q23. Why do the expectations of citizens increase in a democracy?
Answer: Democracy gives citizens the assurance of equality, freedom, and rights. Therefore, citizens start expecting more from the government in terms of better governance, development, employment, education, health, and social justice.
Q24. What can be the state of economic inequality in a democracy?
Answer: Democracy provides political equality, but it is not able to completely eliminate economic inequality. The gap in income and wealth between the rich and the poor may persist in society.
Q25. What is meant by checks and balances?
Answer: In a democracy, various political parties, institutions, and groups criticise and monitor the decisions of the government. This maintains checks and balances to prevent the misuse of power.
Q26. Under what circumstances does democracy accommodate social diversities?
Answer: Democracy successfully accommodates social diversities when the majority group does not discriminate against minorities and the needs and rights of all groups are respected.
Q27. How does democracy accommodate social diversity?
Answer: Democracy gives all citizens equal political rights and provides various social, linguistic, religious, and cultural groups the opportunity to express their views and obtain political representation.
Q28. What is meant by the dignity of citizens/individuals?
Answer: The dignity of citizens means that every person has the right to live life with respect, equality, and freedom, and without facing discrimination.
Q29. How does democracy enhance the dignity of citizens?
Answer: Democracy gives all citizens equal political rights and tries to reduce discrimination based on caste, religion, gender, etc. This increases the dignity and self-respect of citizens.
Q30. What are civil liberties?
Answer: Civil liberties are the freedoms by which a person can express their views, hold peaceful assemblies, form organisations, and exercise their rights.
Q31. How does democracy give freedom to citizens?
Answer: Democracy provides citizens with rights such as freedom of thought and expression, peaceful assembly, forming organisations, and electing representatives of their choice.
Q32. What is the role of democracy in giving equal rights to women?
Answer: Democracy provides women with political and civil rights equal to those of men. They get equal opportunities to vote, contest elections, obtain education, and participate in public life.
Q33. Who was Jayaprakash Narayan?
Answer: Jayaprakash Narayan was a prominent Indian freedom fighter, socialist thinker, and leader who supported democracy. He took part in the freedom movement and later played an important role in upholding democratic values and opposing corruption.
