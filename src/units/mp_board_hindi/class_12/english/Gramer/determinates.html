1. Each and Every का प्रयोग

Each और Every दोनों का हिन्दी अर्थ “प्रत्येक / हर” होता है। दोनों का प्रयोग singular countable noun से पहले किया जाता है।

Each का प्रयोग सामान्यतः दो या दो से अधिक निश्चित व्यक्तियों या वस्तुओं को एक-एक करके बताने के लिए किया जाता है।

Examples:

  1. Each day seemed to pass very slowly.
  2. Cricket is played between two teams. Each team has eleven players.
  3. There are fifteen students in the class. Each student has a bag.
  4. I met two boys yesterday. Each boy was poor.
  5. Each of the students desires to get 90% marks in the examination.
  6. Each room of this hotel has a cooler.

Every का प्रयोग दो या दो से अधिक अनिश्चित व्यक्तियों या वस्तुओं के समूह के प्रत्येक सदस्य के लिए किया जाता है।

Examples:

  1. Every religion, when followed faithfully, leads to God.
  2. The sun rises in the east Every day.
  3. Every room of this hotel has a cooler.
  4. Everyone does not read a newspaper.
  5. I go to the temple Every day.

  4. Ritu gets the train at half past seven every morning.
  5. There were cars parked along each street in the town.
  6. The police questioned each person in the building.
  7. The sun rises in the east every day.
2. Many and Much का प्रयोग

Many और Much दोनों का हिन्दी अर्थ “अनेक / बहुत” होता है।

Many का प्रयोग plural countable nouns के पहले किया जाता है। इससे वस्तुओं या व्यक्तियों की संख्या का बोध होता है।

Examples:

  1. I have many pens to write with.
  2. He ate many fruits yesterday.
  3. He speaks many different languages.
  4. How many pages are there in this book?
  5. We can't buy many things with a little money.
  6. Many people came to see the match.
  7. She can speak many languages.

Much का प्रयोग singular uncountable nouns के पहले किया जाता है। इससे मात्रा का बोध होता है।

Examples:

  1. He has much tea in his cup.
  2. I have much money in my pocket.
  3. There is not much petrol in my bike.
  4. I have eaten much fruit.
  5. He has got much experience.
  6. How much milk is there in the bottle?
  7. How much money have you spent on the birthday party?
  8. The students make much noise in the classroom.
  9. I had to face much trouble.
Exercise: Fill in Many or Much
  1. There is not much milk in the jug.
  2. Have you read many English poems?
  3. He does not have as many friends as I have.
  4. She does not have much money to spend on her daughter's marriage.
  5. How much time do you take to reach the school?
  6. He had too much work to do.
  7. I don't know how many things he has stolen.
3. Some and Any का प्रयोग

Some और Any का हिन्दी अर्थ क्रमशः “कुछ” तथा “कोई / कुछ” होता है। दोनों का प्रयोग countable plural तथा uncountable nouns के साथ किया जा सकता है।

Some का प्रयोग सामान्यतः affirmative sentences तथा विनम्र निवेदन या प्रस्ताव वाले प्रश्नवाचक वाक्यों में किया जाता है।

Examples:

  1. There are some birds sitting on the branches.
  2. Some thieves stole my money yesterday.
  3. Would you give me some money?
  4. Could you get me some coffee?
  5. Will you give me some advice on this matter?
  6. Have some of you ever seen a bird?

Any का प्रयोग सामान्यतः interrogative और negative sentences में किया जाता है।

Examples:

  1. Did you give me any help?
  2. Is there any letter for me?
  3. There isn't any letter for me.
  4. I haven't got any matches.
  5. I have hardly any money.
  6. He had scarcely any food to eat.

Without any का अर्थ “बिना किसी” होता है।

  1. I passed high school without any difficulty.
  2. I climbed up the tree without any help.

4. Either and Neither का प्रयोग

Either का अर्थ “दो में से कोई एक” होता है। इसका प्रयोग दो व्यक्तियों या वस्तुओं में से किसी एक के लिए किया जाता है। इसके बाद आमतौर पर singular noun और singular verb का प्रयोग होता है।

Examples:

  1. Ram can write with either hand.
  2. I haven't read either of these books.
  3. I don't like either of them.
  4. Come on Tuesday or Thursday. Either day is OK.

Neither का अर्थ “दोनों में से कोई नहीं” होता है। इसके बाद आमतौर पर singular noun और singular verb का प्रयोग होता है।

Examples:

  1. She tried both the keys, but neither worked.
  2. Neither of them knows the way.
  3. Neither answer is correct.
  4. Neither of the boys is present.
5. All, Whole and Both का प्रयोग

All का अर्थ “सभी / पूरा” होता है। इसका प्रयोग plural countable nouns तथा uncountable nouns के पहले किया जाता है।

  1. All the students are present.
  2. All the books are useful.
  3. All the water has been used.
  4. All the money was lost.
  5. All that glitters is not gold.
  6. He lost all the money he had.

Whole का अर्थ “पूरा” होता है। इसका प्रयोग सामान्यतः singular countable noun के पहले किया जाता है।

  1. I spent the whole day at home.
  2. She read the whole book.
  3. He worked the whole night.

Both का अर्थ “दोनों” होता है। इसका प्रयोग दो व्यक्तियों या वस्तुओं को बताने के लिए किया जाता है।

  1. Both boys are intelligent.
  2. Both the books are useful.
  3. Both of them are my friends.
6. A Few and A Little का प्रयोग

A few का अर्थ “कुछ / थोड़े” होता है। इसका प्रयोग plural countable nouns के साथ किया जाता है।

  1. I have already spent the few rupees I had.
  2. He needed a few days' rest.
  3. He read a few books from his library.
  4. Only a few boys were present in the class.

A little का अर्थ “थोड़ा” होता है। इसका प्रयोग uncountable nouns के साथ किया जाता है।

  1. A little knowledge is a dangerous thing.
  2. A little money is needed to buy a newspaper.
  3. I have a little water in the bottle.
7. No का प्रयोग

No का अर्थ “कोई नहीं / बिल्कुल नहीं” होता है। इसका प्रयोग singular countable, plural countable तथा uncountable nouns के पहले किया जा सकता है।

  1. I have no spare pen.
  2. I have no right to say so.
  3. The weather was cold, but there was no wind.
  4. There is no water in the tank.

Example: We keep our bread in the fridge so that it does not go bad.

हिंदी: हम अपनी ब्रेड को फ्रिज में रखते हैं ताकि वह खराब न हो जाए।

8. Between and Among का प्रयोग

Between और Among दोनों का हिन्दी अर्थ सामान्यतः “बीच में” होता है।

Between का प्रयोग सामान्यतः दो व्यक्तियों या वस्तुओं के बीच संबंध बताने के लिए किया जाता है।

Examples:

  1. There is a difference between Ram and Shyam.
  2. The agreement was made between the two countries.
  3. The teacher divided the work between the two students.

Among का प्रयोग तीन या तीन से अधिक व्यक्तियों या वस्तुओं के समूह के बीच संबंध बताने के लिए किया जाता है।

Examples:

  1. He was sitting among his friends.
  2. The teacher distributed the books among the students.
  3. There was a quarrel among the three brothers.
⭐ याद रखें:
Between → दो के बीच
Among → दो से अधिक के बीच
