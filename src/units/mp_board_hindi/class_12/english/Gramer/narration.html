Narration (Direct and Indirect Speech)

Direct Speech में वक्ता के वास्तविक शब्द Inverted Commas (" ") के भीतर लिखे जाते हैं।

Indirect Speech में उन्हीं शब्दों को किसी अन्य व्यक्ति द्वारा अपने शब्दों में व्यक्त किया जाता है।

Example :

Direct : Ram said, "I come to school by bike."

Indirect : Ram said that he came to school by bike.

Kinds of Sentences
  1. Assertive Sentences
  2. Interrogative Sentences
  3. Imperative Sentences
  4. Exclamatory Sentences
  5. Optative Sentences
Assertive Sentences - Rule 1 (Change of Reporting Verb)

Assertive Sentences में सामान्यतः said to को told में बदलकर that लगाया जाता है।

Structure :

Direct : Subject + said to + Object + Reported Speech

Indirect : Subject + told + Object + that + Reported Speech

Examples :

Direct : He said to me, "I am busy."

Indirect : He told me that he was busy.

Direct : She said to him, "I have completed my work."

Indirect : She told him that she had completed her work.

Assertive Sentences - Rule 2 (Rules for Change of Words Expressing Nearness of Time or Position)

Indirect Speech में समय एवं स्थान की निकटता (Nearness of Time and Position) बताने वाले शब्दों में निम्न परिवर्तन किये जाते हैं।

Direct Speech Indirect Speech
Now Then
This That
These Those
Here There
Ago Before
Today That Day
Yesterday The Previous Day
Tomorrow The Next Day
Tonight That Night
Last Night The Previous Night
Next Week The Following Week
Next Month The Following Month
Next Year The Following Year

Examples :

Direct : He said to them, "I will leave you now."

Indirect : He told them that he would leave them then.

Direct : Ritesh said to Komal, "I cannot go with you till next Monday."

Indirect : Ritesh told Komal that he could not go with her till the following Monday.

Direct : He said to me, "I came here yesterday."

Indirect : He told me that he had come there the previous day.

Examples :

Direct : He said, "We are playing cricket now."

Indirect : He said that they were playing cricket then.

Direct : She said, "I can sing a song."

Indirect : She said that she could sing a song.

Direct : They said, "We have won the match."

Indirect : They said that they had won the match.

Direct : She said, "We will complete this work today."

Indirect : She said that they would complete that work that day.

Assertive Sentences - Rule 3 (Universal Truth, Proverb and Historical Fact)

यदि Reported Speech में Universal Truth, Habitual Fact, Proverb या Historical Fact हो तो Tense में कोई परिवर्तन नहीं किया जाता।

Examples :

Direct : The teacher said, "The sun rises in the east."

Indirect : The teacher said that the sun rises in the east.

Direct : The teacher said, "The earth moves round the sun."

Indirect : The teacher said that the earth moves round the sun.

Direct : He said, "Honesty is the best policy."

Indirect : He said that honesty is the best policy.

Direct : The history teacher said, "Samudra Gupta is called the Napoleon of India."

Indirect : The history teacher said that Samudra Gupta is called the Napoleon of India.

Special Rule 1 (Change of Pronouns)

First Person → Reporting Verb के Subject के अनुसार बदलता है।

Second Person → Reporting Verb के Object के अनुसार बदलता है।

Third Person → कोई परिवर्तन नहीं होता।

Person Rule
First Person According to Subject
Second Person According to Object
Third Person No Change

Examples :

Direct : He said to me, "I do not like that book."

Indirect : He told me that he did not like that book.

Direct : He said to me, "You are happy."

Indirect : He told me that I was happy.

Direct : He said to me, "She is a good girl."

Indirect : He told me that she was a good girl.

Indirect : He told me that he had come there the previous day.

Interrogative Sentences - Rule 1 (Wh- Family Questions)

यदि प्रश्नवाचक वाक्य What, Who, When, Where, Why, Which, Whose, How आदि से प्रारम्भ हो तो इन्हें Indirect Speech में भी यथावत रखा जाता है।

said to को asked में बदला जाता है तथा that का प्रयोग नहीं किया जाता।

Structure :

Direct : Subject + said to + Object + Wh-word + Question ?

Indirect : Subject + asked + Object + Wh-word + Statement

Examples :

Direct : She said to me, "Where are you going?"

Indirect : She asked me where I was going.

Direct : She said to me, "What are you doing?"

Indirect : She asked me what I was doing.

Direct : The teacher said to the student, "Where were you yesterday?"

Indirect : The teacher asked the student where he had been the previous day.

Direct : He said to me, "Why are you absent today?"

Indirect : He asked me why I was absent that day.

Interrogative Sentences - Rule 2 (Yes / No Type Questions)

यदि प्रश्न Helping Verb से प्रारम्भ हो तथा उत्तर Yes या No में दिया जा सकता हो, तो Indirect Speech में if अथवा whether का प्रयोग किया जाता है।

said to को asked में बदला जाता है।

Structure :

Direct : Subject + said to + Object + Helping Verb + Subject + Verb ?

Indirect : Subject + asked + Object + if / whether + Subject + Verb

Examples :

Direct : She said to me, "Do you know the way?"

Indirect : She asked me whether I knew the way.

Direct : The teacher said to me, "Will you come tomorrow?"

Indirect : The teacher asked me whether I would come the next day.

Direct : She said to me, "Are you studying now?"

Indirect : She asked me whether I was studying then.

Direct : He said to me, "Do you like cricket?"

Indirect : He asked me whether I liked cricket.

Imperative Sentences - Rule 1 (Order / Command)

यदि वाक्य में आदेश (Order) या Command हो तो Reporting Verb को ordered अथवा commanded में बदलते हैं तथा to + Verb का प्रयोग करते हैं।

Structure :

Subject + ordered + Object + to + Verb

Examples :

Direct : The officer said to the soldier, "March on."

Indirect : The officer ordered the soldier to march on.

Direct : The teacher said to the students, "Keep quiet."

Indirect : The teacher ordered the students to keep quiet.

Imperative Sentences - Rule 2 (Request)

यदि वाक्य में Request हो तो Reporting Verb को requested में बदलते हैं।

Structure :

Subject + requested + Object + to + Verb

Examples :

Direct : He said to me, "Please help me."

Indirect : He requested me to help him.

Direct : She said to the teacher, "Please explain this topic again."

Indirect : She requested the teacher to explain that topic again.

Imperative Sentences - Rule 3 (Advice)

यदि वाक्य में सलाह (Advice) दी गई हो तो Reporting Verb को advised में बदलते हैं।

Structure :

Subject + advised + Object + to + Verb

Examples :

Direct : The teacher said to the students, "Work hard."

Indirect : The teacher advised the students to work hard.

Direct : My father said to me, "Respect your elders."

Indirect : My father advised me to respect my elders.

Imperative Sentences - Rule 4 (Negative Commands)

यदि Imperative Sentence Negative हो तो not to + Verb का प्रयोग किया जाता है।

Structure :

Subject + ordered/advised/requested + Object + not to + Verb

Examples :

Direct : The teacher said to the students, "Do not waste your time."

Indirect : The teacher advised the students not to waste their time.

Direct : The doctor said to him, "Do not smoke."

Indirect : The doctor advised him not to smoke.

Imperative Sentences - Special Rule (Let)

यदि वाक्य Let से प्रारम्भ हो तो भाव के अनुसार advised, requested, proposed आदि का प्रयोग किया जाता है।

Examples :

Direct : He said, "Let us go for a walk."

Indirect : He proposed that they should go for a walk.

Direct : The teacher said, "Let us start the class."

Indirect : The teacher proposed that they should start the class.
