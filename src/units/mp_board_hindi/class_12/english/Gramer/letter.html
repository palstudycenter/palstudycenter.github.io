Question - Vivek Sharma, living at 45 D, Vijay Colony, New Delhi, writes a letter to the Municipal Commissioner of Delhi complaining about the insanitary conditions in your locality.

Answer -

45 D, Vijay Colony
New Delhi

10 July, 2026

To,
The Municipal Commissioner,
Municipal Corporation,
New Delhi

Subject: Complaint about the insanitary conditions in my locality.

Sir,

I wish to draw your kind attention to the insanitary conditions in our locality, Vijay Colony. The drains are blocked and overflowing. Garbage is lying on the roads for many days. Dirty water has collected in many places, which has become a breeding place for mosquitoes and flies. The foul smell has made the environment unhealthy. There is a danger of diseases like dengue and malaria.

The residents have complained many times, but no action has been taken. I request you to take immediate steps to clean the drains, remove the garbage, and spray disinfectants regularly.

I hope you will look into the matter and take prompt action.

Thanking you.

Yours faithfully,
Vivek Sharma

Question - You are Aman Sharma residing at 60 Nehru Colony, Dewas. Write a letter to the Post Master complaining against the Postman of your area.

Answer -

60, Nehru Colony
Dewas (M.P.)

10 July, 2026

To,
The Post Master,
Head Post Office,
Dewas (M.P.)

Subject: Complaint against the postman of our area.

Sir,

I wish to draw your kind attention to the careless and irresponsible behaviour of the postman in our area. He does not deliver letters and parcels on time. Sometimes he delivers letters to the wrong addresses, and many important documents reach us very late. He also behaves rudely with the residents when they ask about their mail.

Due to his negligence, the people of our locality are facing great inconvenience. Important letters, bills, and official documents are often delayed.

I, therefore, request you to look into the matter and take necessary action against the postman. Kindly ensure that postal services in our locality are regular and efficient.

Thanking you.

Yours faithfully,
Aman Sharma

Question - You are Sanjay Singh living at Mahatma Gandhi Hostel, Indore (M.P.). Write a letter to your father asking him to send Rs. 5000/- for purchasing new books and informing him about hostel food quality.

Answer -

Mahatma Gandhi Hostel
Indore (M.P.)

10 July, 2026

Dear Father,

I hope you are fine and in good health. I am also fine here and concentrating on my studies.

Our new academic session has started, and I need to purchase some new textbooks and reference books. Therefore, kindly send me Rs. 5000/- at the earliest so that I can buy the required books without any delay.

The hostel is comfortable, but the quality of food is not satisfactory. Sometimes the food is not fresh, and it lacks proper nutrition. The hostel authorities have been informed, and I hope they will improve the food quality soon.

Yours lovingly,
Sanjay Singh

Question - Write a letter to the Editor of a newspaper drawing attention towards the acute shortage of water in your city.

Answer -

45, Shastri Nagar
Bhopal (M.P.)

10 July, 2026

To,
The Editor,
The Times of India,
Bhopal (M.P.)

Subject: Acute shortage of water in the city.

Sir,

Through the columns of your esteemed newspaper, I wish to draw the attention of the concerned authorities towards the acute shortage of water in our city. The residents are facing great difficulty due to the irregular water supply. In many areas, people do not get sufficient drinking water for several days.

The situation becomes worse during the summer season. People have to stand in long queues to collect water from public taps. This shortage affects daily life and creates serious health and sanitation problems.

I request the concerned authorities to take immediate steps to improve the water supply, repair leaking pipelines, and promote rainwater harvesting to overcome this problem.

I hope this issue will be highlighted in your newspaper so that the authorities take prompt action.

Thanking you.

Yours faithfully,
XYZ
