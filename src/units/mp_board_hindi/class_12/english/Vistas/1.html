Lesson 1 – The Third Level

📖 परिचय

Q1. What does the third level refer to?
Ans (English): The third level refers to an imaginary floor at Grand Central Station. It symbolizes Charley’s wish to escape from the modern world into the peaceful past.
उत्तर (Hindi): तीसरा स्तर ग्रैंड सेंट्रल स्टेशन का एक काल्पनिक भाग है। यह चार्ली की उस इच्छा का प्रतीक है जिसमें वह आधुनिक जीवन से भागकर अतीत की शांत दुनिया में जाना चाहता है।
Q2. Would Charley ever go back to the ticket counter on the third level to buy tickets to Galesburg for himself and his wife?
Ans (English): No, Charley would never go back to the ticket counter because the third level was only his imagination.
उत्तर (Hindi): नहीं, चार्ली कभी भी टिकट काउंटर पर वापस नहीं जा सकता क्योंकि तीसरा स्तर केवल उसकी कल्पना था।
Q3. Do you think that the third level was a medium of escape for Charley? Why?
Ans (English): Yes, it was a medium of escape for Charley. He imagined it to get rid of stress, fear, and insecurity of the modern world and to enter the peaceful life of 1894.
उत्तर (Hindi): हाँ, यह चार्ली के लिए पलायन का माध्यम था। उसने इसे आधुनिक जीवन के भय, तनाव और असुरक्षा से बचकर 1894 के शांत जीवन में जाने के लिए कल्पित किया।
Q4. What do you infer from Sam’s letter to Charley?
Ans (English): Sam’s letter suggests that he too escaped into the past. It shows Charley’s strong belief in the existence of the third level.
उत्तर (Hindi): सैम का पत्र बताता है कि वह भी अतीत में चला गया। यह चार्ली के तीसरे स्तर पर विश्वास को मजबूत करता है।
Q5. How does “The Third Level” illustrate the theme of escapism?
Ans (English): The story illustrates escapism as Charley creates an imaginary third level to avoid the tensions of the modern world. He wishes to go to Galesburg of 1894 for peace and comfort.
उत्तर (Hindi): यह कहानी पलायनवाद को दर्शाती है क्योंकि चार्ली आधुनिक जीवन के तनाव से बचने के लिए एक काल्पनिक तीसरे स्तर की रचना करता है। वह शांति और सुकून के लिए 1894 के गैल्सबर्ग जाना चाहता है।

📚 Reading with Insight

Q3. “The modern world is full of insecurity, fear, war, worry and stress.” What are the ways in which we attempt to overcome them?
Ans (English): People try to escape through imagination, nostalgia, hobbies, and creative activities like reading, painting, or music. These give relief from stress.
उत्तर (Hindi): लोग कल्पना, अतीत की यादों, शौक और रचनात्मक कार्यों जैसे पढ़ना, चित्रकला या संगीत द्वारा तनाव से मुक्ति पाने की कोशिश करते हैं।
Q4. Do you see an intersection of time and space in the story?
Ans (English): Yes, the story shows the intersection of time and space. The present-day station coexists with the world of 1894 on the third level.
उत्तर (Hindi): हाँ, कहानी में समय और स्थान का संगम दिखाई देता है। वर्तमान का स्टेशन तीसरे स्तर पर 1894 की दुनिया के साथ जुड़ा हुआ है।
Q5. Apparent illogicality sometimes turns out to be a futuristic projection? Discuss.
Ans (English): Yes, many ideas that seem illogical today may become real in the future. The third level may represent such a futuristic imagination.
उत्तर (Hindi): हाँ, कई बातें जो आज अव्यावहारिक लगती हैं, भविष्य में सच हो सकती हैं। तीसरा स्तर भी ऐसी ही भविष्य की कल्पना को दर्शा सकता है।

📝 Extract Based Questions – A

Extract A
“The presidents of the New York Central and the New York, New Heaven and Hartford railroads will swear on a stack of timetables that there are only two. But I say there are three, because I've been on the third level of the Grand Central Station. Yes I've taken the obvious step: talked to a psychiatrist friend of mine, among others. I told him about the third level at Grand Central Station and he said it was a waking-dream wish fulfillment. He said I was unhappy. That made my wife kind of mad, but he explained that he meant the modern world is full of insecurity, fear, war, worry and all the rest of it, and that I just want to escape. Well, who doesn't? Everybody I know wants to escape, but they don't wander down into any third level at Grand Central Station.”
1. How many levels are there in the Grand Central?
(a) Two
(b) Three
(c) One
(d) Four
✔ Correct Answer: (a) Two
2. Who is the central character of the story?
(a) Marley
(b) Charley
(c) Harley
(d) Darley
✔ Correct Answer: (b) Charley
3. Why did the author say that there are three levels?
(a) He heard from others
(b) Timetables say so
(c) There are actually 3 levels
(d) He has been to level three
✔ Correct Answer: (d) He has been to level three
4. According to Sam, why did Charley wish to escape?
(a) His wife was mad
(b) He wished to go to third level
(c) He wanted to meet his grandfather
(d) He wanted to get away from insecurities
✔ Correct Answer: (d) He wanted to get away from insecurities

📝 Extract Based Questions – B

Extract B
“To make sure, I walked over to a newsboy and glanced at the stack of papers at his feet. It was The World; and The World hasn't been published for years. The lead story said something about President Cleveland. I've found that front page since, in the Public Library files, and it was printed June 11, 1894. I turned towards the ticket windows knowing that here on the third level at Grand Central—I could buy tickets that would take Louisa and me anywhere in the United States we wanted to go. In the year 1894. And I wanted two tickets to Galesburg, Illinois.”
1. What was the name of the newspaper?
(a) Grand Central
(b) President Cleveland
(c) Galesburg
(d) The World
✔ Correct Answer: (d) The World
2. Where did Charley want to take his wife?
(a) Galesburg, Illinois
(b) The Third Level
(c) The Grand Central
(d) Into the Past
✔ Correct Answer: (a) Galesburg, Illinois
3. The newspaper was printed on:
(a) 17.06.1894
(b) 11.06.1894
(c) 11.06.1984
(d) 06.11.1984
✔ Correct Answer: (b) 11.06.1894
4. Give the opposite of 'lost' from the passage.
(a) front
(b) turned
(c) found
(d) stack
✔ Correct Answer: (c) found
