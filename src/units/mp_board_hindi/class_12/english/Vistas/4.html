पाल स्टडी सेंटर

Lesson 4 – The Enemy

Class 12 English | MP Board

Written by Pearl S. Buck

📖 परिचय

Q1. Who was Dr. Sadao? Where was his house?
Ans (English): Dr. Sadao Hoki was a famous Japanese surgeon and scientist. He had studied surgery and medicine in America. His house was on the Japanese coast, on rocks above a narrow beach.
उत्तर (Hindi): डॉ. सदाओ होकी एक प्रसिद्ध जापानी सर्जन और वैज्ञानिक थे। उन्होंने अमेरिका में सर्जरी और चिकित्सा की पढ़ाई की थी। उनका घर जापान के समुद्र तट पर एक संकरे समुद्र तट से ऊपर चट्टानों पर बना था।
Q2. Why was Dr. Sadao not sent with the Japan army?
Ans (English): Dr. Sadao was not sent with the troops because he was perfecting a discovery that would make wounds completely clean. Moreover, the old General might need an operation, so Sadao was kept in Japan.
उत्तर (Hindi): डॉ. सदाओ को सेना के साथ नहीं भेजा गया क्योंकि वे ऐसी खोज को पूरा कर रहे थे जिससे घावों को पूरी तरह साफ किया जा सके। इसके अलावा, वृद्ध जनरल को ऑपरेशन की आवश्यकता पड़ सकती थी, इसलिए सदाओ को जापान में रखा गया।
Q3. In what condition did Dr. Sadao and Hana find the enemy soldier?
Ans (English): Dr. Sadao and Hana found the wounded American prisoner lying near the shore. He was badly wounded and weak, and he had been shot.
उत्तर (Hindi): डॉ. सदाओ और हाना ने घायल अमेरिकी सैनिक को समुद्र तट के पास पड़ा हुआ पाया। वह बहुत घायल और कमजोर था तथा उसे गोली लगी थी।
Q4. Will Hana help the wounded man and wash him herself?
Ans (English): Yes, Hana helped the wounded man. Although he was an enemy, she showed humanity and washed him herself when the servants refused to do so.
उत्तर (Hindi): हाँ, हाना ने घायल व्यक्ति की मदद की। यद्यपि वह एक दुश्मन सैनिक था, फिर भी हाना ने मानवता दिखाते हुए उसकी मदद की और नौकरों के मना करने पर स्वयं उसे साफ किया।
Q5. What did Dr. Sadao and his wife do with the wounded man?
Ans (English): Dr. Sadao and Hana decided to save the wounded man's life. Sadao operated on him and removed the bullet, while Hana helped him and cared for him.
उत्तर (Hindi): डॉ. सदाओ और हाना ने घायल व्यक्ति की जान बचाने का निर्णय लिया। सदाओ ने उसका ऑपरेशन करके गोली निकाली, जबकि हाना ने उसकी देखभाल की।
Q6. Why did Dr. Sadao's servants leave his service?
Ans (English): The servants left because they considered the wounded American to be an enemy of Japan. They did not approve of Sadao and Hana treating and sheltering him.
उत्तर (Hindi): नौकरों ने इसलिए नौकरी छोड़ दी क्योंकि वे घायल अमेरिकी को जापान का दुश्मन मानते थे। उन्हें सदाओ और हाना द्वारा उसकी चिकित्सा करने और उसे घर में रखने की बात पसंद नहीं थी।
Q7. What will Dr. Sadao do to get rid of the man?
Ans (English): Dr. Sadao decided to help the prisoner escape from Japan. He planned to put him on a boat and guide him towards a nearby island from where he could escape safely.
उत्तर (Hindi): डॉ. सदाओ ने कैदी को जापान से भागने में मदद करने का निर्णय लिया। उन्होंने उसे नाव में बैठाकर एक निकटवर्ती द्वीप की ओर भेजने की योजना बनाई, जहाँ से वह सुरक्षित रूप से भाग सकता था।
Q8. Will Dr. Sadao be arrested on the charge of harbouring an enemy?
Ans (English): No, Dr. Sadao was not arrested. Although he had sheltered an enemy soldier, the General trusted him and needed his medical services. The prisoner was eventually helped to escape.
उत्तर (Hindi): नहीं, डॉ. सदाओ को गिरफ्तार नहीं किया गया। यद्यपि उन्होंने एक दुश्मन सैनिक को शरण दी थी, लेकिन जनरल को उन पर विश्वास था और उनकी चिकित्सा सेवाओं की आवश्यकता थी। अंततः कैदी को भागने में मदद की गई।
Q9. How did Dr. Sadao help the prisoner escape?
Ans (English): Dr. Sadao gave the prisoner a boat, food and other necessary things. He instructed him to escape towards a nearby island and wait for a Korean fishing boat. Later, Sadao realized that the prisoner had escaped.
उत्तर (Hindi): डॉ. सदाओ ने कैदी को नाव, भोजन और आवश्यक सामान दिया। उन्होंने उसे पास के एक द्वीप की ओर जाने और कोरियाई मछली पकड़ने वाली नाव की प्रतीक्षा करने को कहा। बाद में सदाओ को पता चला कि कैदी भाग चुका था।

⭐ Important Extra Questions

Q10. What conflict did Dr. Sadao face in the story?
Ans (English): Dr. Sadao faced a conflict between his duty as a Japanese citizen and his duty as a doctor. As a patriot, he considered the man an enemy, but as a doctor, he could not allow a wounded person to die.
उत्तर (Hindi): डॉ. सदाओ को जापानी नागरिक के कर्तव्य और डॉक्टर के कर्तव्य के बीच संघर्ष का सामना करना पड़ा। एक देशभक्त के रूप में वह उस व्यक्ति को दुश्मन मानते थे, लेकिन डॉक्टर होने के कारण वे एक घायल व्यक्ति को मरने नहीं दे सकते थे।
Q11. What message does the story “The Enemy” give?
Ans (English): The story gives the message that humanity is above hatred and war. A doctor’s first duty is to save a human life, irrespective of nationality or enemy status.
उत्तर (Hindi): कहानी यह संदेश देती है कि मानवता घृणा और युद्ध से ऊपर है। डॉक्टर का पहला कर्तव्य मानव जीवन को बचाना है, चाहे व्यक्ति किसी भी देश का हो या दुश्मन ही क्यों न हो।

📝 Extract Based Questions – A

Extract A
Dr. Sadao Hoki's house was built on a spot of the Japanese coast where as a little boy he had often played. The low, square stone house was set upon rocks well above a narrow beach that was outlined with bent pines. As a boy Sadao had climbed the pines, supporting himself on his bare feet, as he had seen men do in the South Seas when they climbed for coconuts.
1. Dr. Sadao Hoki's house was built:
(a) In plains
(b) Near a coast
(c) At the top of a hill
(d) In the forest
✔ Correct Answer: (b) Near a coast
2. Which trees outlined Dr. Sadao Hoki's house?
(a) Pine
(b) Coconut
(c) Apple
(d) Guava
✔ Correct Answer: (a) Pine
3. The antonym of the word 'narrow' is:
(a) Tight
(b) Heavy
(c) Broad

(a) The Enemy

📝 Extract Based Questions – B

Extract B
Sadao knew that his education was his father's chief concern. For this reason he had been sent at twenty-two to America to learn all that could be learned of surgery and medicine. He had come back at thirty, and before his father died he had seen Sadao become famous not only as a surgeon but as a scientist. Because he was perfecting a discovery which would render wounds entirely clean, he had not been sent abroad with the troops. Also, he knew, there was some slight danger that the old General might need an operation for a condition for which he was now being treated medically, and for this possibility Sadao was being kept in Japan.
1. What was the chief concern of Dr. Sadao's father?
(a) Dr. Sadao's education
(b) Dr. Sadao's treatment
(c) Dr. Sadao's marriage
(d) Dr. Sadao's children
✔ Correct Answer: (a) Dr. Sadao's education
2. At what age did Dr. Sadao go to America?
(a) Thirty
(b) Twenty-two
(c) Twenty
(d) Thirty-two
✔ Correct Answer: (b) Twenty-two
3. Why was Dr. Sadao sent to America?
(a) To learn surgery and medicine
(b) To learn scientific activities
(c) To learn about wounds
(d) For further studies
✔ Correct Answer: (a) To learn surgery and medicine
4. Find the word from the extract which is opposite in meaning to 'fraction'.
(a) Army
(b) Unit

(d) Group
✔ Correct Answer: (d) Group
