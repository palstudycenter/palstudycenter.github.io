📘 Lesson 1- The Last Lesson
Q.1 Who was M. Hamel?
M. Hamel was the French teacher of Alsace school who taught sincerely for forty years.
Q.2 Who was Franz?
Franz was a careless student of M. Hamel who later realized the value of learning.
Q.3 What was Franz expected to be prepared with for school that day?
He was expected to prepare the rules of participles in French grammar.
Q.4 What did Franz notice that was unusual about the school that day?
He noticed unusual silence and villagers sitting in the classroom.
Q.5 What had been put up on the bulletin board?
A notice from Berlin that only German would be taught in Alsace and Lorraine.
Q.6 What changes did the order from Berlin cause in school that day?
The school became silent and villagers came to attend the last French lesson.
Q.7 How did Franz's feeling about M. Hamel and school change?
He developed respect for M. Hamel and regretted neglecting his studies.
Q.8 What was the dress of M. Hamel on the last day?
He wore a green coat, frilled shirt, and black silk cap.
Q.9 What did M. Hamel say about French language?
He said French was the most beautiful and logical language, a key to freedom.
Q.10 Why was Franz afraid to go to school?
Franz was afraid to go to school because he had not prepared the lesson on participles.
Q.11 Why did Franz think of spending the day outdoors?
He thought of spending the day outdoors because the weather was warm and pleasant and he had not prepared his lesson.
Q.12 Why were the villagers sitting in the classroom?
The villagers were sitting in the classroom to thank M. Hamel for his forty years of service and to attend the last French lesson.
Q.13 What announcement did M. Hamel make?
M. Hamel announced that it was his last lesson and that only German would be taught from the next day.
Q.14 Why did Franz regret not learning his lessons?
Franz regretted not learning his lessons because he realized that he would never get another chance to learn French.
Q.15 How did M. Hamel blame himself for the neglect of learning?
M. Hamel blamed himself because he often gave students holidays and sent them to water his flowers instead of teaching them.
Q.16 What did M. Hamel write on the blackboard at the end of the lesson?
M. Hamel wrote "Vive La France" on the blackboard at the end of the lesson.
Q.17 What does "Vive La France" mean?
"Vive La France" means "Long Live France".
Q.18 What lesson do we learn from the chapter?
We learn the importance of language, education, culture, and making proper use of opportunities.
