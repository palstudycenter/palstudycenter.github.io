पाल स्टडी सेंटर
📘 Lesson 2- Lost Spring
Q.1 Who is Saheb?
Saheb is a poor ragpicker boy living in Seemapuri, symbolising poverty and lost childhood.
Q.2 What was the full name of Saheb?
His full name is Saheb-e-Alam, which ironically means "Lord of the Universe".
Q.3 What is Saheb looking for in the garbage dumps?
He searches for coins, food, and small valuables which give him hope of survival.
Q.4 Is Saheb happy working at the tea stall?
No, he feels trapped because he lost his independence and childhood freedom.
Q.5 What explanation does the author offer for the children not wearing footwear?
The author says it is due to poverty and also considered a tradition by some families.
Q.6 What makes the city of Firozabad famous?
Firozabad is world-famous for its glass bangles industry.
Q.7 Mention the hazards of working in the glass bangle industry.
It causes blindness, lung diseases, low wages, exploitation, and lifelong poverty.
Q.8 How is Mukesh’s attitude to his situation different from that of his family?
Mukesh dreams of becoming a motor mechanic, unlike his family who accept bangle-making as destiny.
Q.9 What could be some of the reasons for the migration of people from villages to cities?
Unemployment, poverty, natural calamities, and the hope of a better livelihood force migration.
Q.10 How in your opinion can Mukesh realise his dream?
By getting training in mechanics, staying determined, and escaping the cycle of poverty.
Q.11 Why should child labour be eliminated and how?
Because it ruins childhood and education. It should end through strict laws, free education, and awareness.
Q.12 Who is Mukesh? What is his dream? How does his dream look like?
Mukesh is a poor boy from Firozabad. His dream is to become a motor mechanic. Though difficult, it shows his courage and hope.
