Q.1 Find the adjoint of the matrix \[ A= \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2\\ 3 & 4 \end{bmatrix} \]

उत्तर -

सहगुणक (Cofactor) मैट्रिक्स —

\[ C= \begin{bmatrix} 4 & -3\\ -2 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \]

Adjoint = Cofactor Matrix का Transpose

\[ \operatorname{adj}(A) = \begin{bmatrix} 4 & -2\\ -3 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \]

Q.2 Find the adjoint of the matrix \[ A= \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -1 & 2\\ 2 & 3 & 5\\ -2 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \]

उत्तर -

सहगुणक (Cofactors) —

\[ C_{11}= \begin{vmatrix} 3 & 5\\ 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix} =3 \]

\[ C_{12}=- \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 5\\ -2 & 1 \end{vmatrix} =-12 \]

\[ C_{13}= \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 3\\ -2 & 0 \end{vmatrix} =6 \]

\[ C_{21}=- \begin{vmatrix} -1 & 2\\ 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix} =1 \]

\[ C_{22}= \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2\\ -2 & 1 \end{vmatrix} =5 \]

\[ C_{23}=- \begin{vmatrix} 1 & -1\\ -2 & 0 \end{vmatrix} =-2 \]

\[ C_{31}= \begin{vmatrix} -1 & 2\\ 3 & 5 \end{vmatrix} =-11 \]

\[ C_{32}=- \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2\\ 2 & 5 \end{vmatrix} =-1 \]

\[ C_{33}= \begin{vmatrix} 1 & -1\\ 2 & 3 \end{vmatrix} =5 \]

\[ Adj(A)= \begin{bmatrix} C_{11} & C_{21} & C_{31}\\ C_{12} & C_{22} & C_{32}\\ C_{13} & C_{23} & C_{33} \end{bmatrix} \]

\[ Adj(A)= \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 1 & -11\\ -12 & 5 & -1\\ 6 & -2 & 5 \end{bmatrix} \]

Q.3 Verify that \[ A(\operatorname{Adj}A)=(\operatorname{Adj}A)A=|A|I \] for \[ A= \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 3\\ -4 & -6 \end{bmatrix} \]

उत्तर -

\[ |A|= \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 3\\ -4 & -6 \end{vmatrix} \]

\[ =(2)(-6)-(3)(-4) \]

\[ =-12+12 \]

\[ =0 \]

\[ Adj(A)= \begin{bmatrix} -6 & -3\\ 4 & 2 \end{bmatrix} \]

\[ A\,Adj(A) = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 3\\ -4 & -6 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} -6 & -3\\ 4 & 2 \end{bmatrix} \]

\[ = \begin{bmatrix} 2(-6)+3(4) & 2(-3)+3(2)\\ -4(-6)+(-6)(4) & -4(-3)+(-6)(2) \end{bmatrix} \]

\[ = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 0\\ 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix} \]

\[ =(0) \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0\\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \]

\[ =|A|I \]

\[ Adj(A)\,A = \begin{bmatrix} -6 & -3\\ 4 & 2 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 3\\ -4 & -6 \end{bmatrix} \]

\[ = \begin{bmatrix} -12+12 & -18+18\\ 8-8 & 12-12 \end{bmatrix} \]

\[ = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 0\\ 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix} \]

\[ =(0) \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0\\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \]

\[ =|A|I \]

\[ \therefore A(\operatorname{Adj}A) =(\operatorname{Adj}A)A =|A|I \]

Q.5 Find the inverse of the matrix \[ A= \begin{bmatrix} 2 & -2\\ 4 & 3 \end{bmatrix} \]

उत्तर -

\[ |A|= \begin{vmatrix} 2 & -2\\ 4 & 3 \end{vmatrix} \]

\[ =(2)(3)-(-2)(4) \]

\[ =6+8 \]

\[ =14 \]

\[ Adj(A)= \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 2\\ -4 & 2 \end{bmatrix} \]

Inverse का सूत्र —

\[ A^{-1}=\frac{1}{|A|}\,Adj(A) \]

\[ A^{-1} =\frac{1}{14} \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 2\\ -4 & 2 \end{bmatrix} \]

\[ A^{-1} = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{3}{14} & \frac{1}{7}\\ -\frac{2}{7} & \frac{1}{7} \end{bmatrix} \]

Q.6 Find the inverse of the matrix \[ A= \begin{bmatrix} -1 & 5\\ -3 & 2 \end{bmatrix} \]

उत्तर -

\[ |A|= \begin{vmatrix} -1 & 5\\ -3 & 2 \end{vmatrix} \]

\[ =(-1)(2)-(5)(-3) \]

\[ =-2+15 \]

\[ =13 \]

\[ Adj(A)= \begin{bmatrix} 2 & -5\\ 3 & -1 \end{bmatrix} \]

Inverse का सूत्र —

\[ A^{-1}=\frac{1}{|A|}\,Adj(A) \]

\[ A^{-1} =\frac{1}{13} \begin{bmatrix} 2 & -5\\ 3 & -1 \end{bmatrix} \]

\[ A^{-1} = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{2}{13} & -\frac{5}{13}\\ \frac{3}{13} & -\frac{1}{13} \end{bmatrix} \]

Q.8 Find the inverse of the matrix \[ A= \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0\\ 3 & 3 & 0\\ 5 & 2 & -1 \end{bmatrix} \]

उत्तर -

\[ |A|= \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0\\ 3 & 3 & 0\\ 5 & 2 & -1 \end{vmatrix} \]

\[ =1 \begin{vmatrix} 3 & 0\\ 2 & -1 \end{vmatrix} \]

\[ =1(3\times -1-0\times 2) \]

\[ =-3 \]

\[ Adj(A)= \begin{bmatrix} -3 & 0 & 0\\ 3 & -1 & 0\\ -9 & 2 & 3 \end{bmatrix} \]

Inverse का सूत्र —

\[ A^{-1}=\frac{1}{|A|}\,Adj(A) \]

\[ A^{-1} =\frac{1}{-3} \begin{bmatrix} -3 & 0 & 0\\ 3 & -1 & 0\\ -9 & 2 & 3 \end{bmatrix} \]

\[ A^{-1} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0\\ -1 & \frac13 & 0\\ 3 & -\frac23 & -1 \end{bmatrix} \]

Q.12 Let \[ A= \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 7\\ 2 & 5 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad B= \begin{bmatrix} 6 & 8\\ 7 & 9 \end{bmatrix} \] Verify that \[ (AB)^{-1}=B^{-1}A^{-1} \]

उत्तर -

\[ AB= \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 7\\ 2 & 5 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 6 & 8\\ 7 & 9 \end{bmatrix} \]

\[ = \begin{bmatrix} 67 & 87\\ 47 & 61 \end{bmatrix} \]

\[ |AB|= \begin{vmatrix} 67 & 87\\ 47 & 61 \end{vmatrix} \]

\[ =(67)(61)-(87)(47) \]

\[ =4087-4089 \]

\[ =-2 \]

\[ (AB)^{-1} =\frac{1}{-2} \begin{bmatrix} 61 & -87\\ -47 & 67 \end{bmatrix} \]

\[ = \begin{bmatrix} -\frac{61}{2} & \frac{87}{2}\\ \frac{47}{2} & -\frac{67}{2} \end{bmatrix} \]

\[ |A|= \begin{vmatrix} 3 & 7\\ 2 & 5 \end{vmatrix} =15-14=1 \]

\[ A^{-1} = \begin{bmatrix} 5 & -7\\ -2 & 3 \end{bmatrix} \]

\[ |B|= \begin{vmatrix} 6 & 8\\ 7 & 9 \end{vmatrix} =54-56=-2 \]

\[ B^{-1} =\frac{1}{-2} \begin{bmatrix} 9 & -8\\ -7 & 6 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} -\frac92 & 4\\ \frac72 & -3 \end{bmatrix} \]

\[ B^{-1}A^{-1} = \begin{bmatrix} -\frac92 & 4\\ \frac72 & -3 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 5 & -7\\ -2 & 3 \end{bmatrix} \]

\[ = \begin{bmatrix} -\frac{61}{2} & \frac{87}{2}\\ \frac{47}{2} & -\frac{67}{2} \end{bmatrix} \]

\[ (AB)^{-1} = B^{-1}A^{-1} \]

\[ \therefore \text{Verified} \]

Q.13 If \[ A= \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 1\\ -1 & 2 \end{bmatrix} \] show that \[ A^2-5A+7I=O \] Hence find \(A^{-1}\).

उत्तर -

\[ A= \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 1\\ -1 & 2 \end{bmatrix} \]

\[ A^2= \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 1\\ -1 & 2 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 1\\ -1 & 2 \end{bmatrix} \]

\[ = \begin{bmatrix} 8 & 5\\ -5 & 3 \end{bmatrix} \]

\[ A^2-5A+7I= \begin{bmatrix} 8 & 5\\ -5 & 3 \end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix} 15 & 5\\ -5 & 10 \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} 7 & 0\\ 0 & 7 \end{bmatrix} \]

\[ = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 0\\ 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix} =O \]

अतः

\[ A^2-5A+7I=O \]

\[ A-5I+7A^{-1}=O \]

\[ A^{-1}=\frac{1}{7}(5I-A) \]

\[ =\frac{1}{7} \left[ \begin{bmatrix} 5 & 0\\ 0 & 5 \end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 1\\ -1 & 2 \end{bmatrix} \right] \]

\[ =\frac{1}{7} \begin{bmatrix} 2 & -1\\ 1 & 3 \end{bmatrix} \]

\[ \therefore A^{-1} = \frac{1}{7} \begin{bmatrix} 2 & -1\\ 1 & 3 \end{bmatrix} \]
