Q.1 (i) Determine whether the relation \[ R=\{(x,y):3x-y=0\} \] defined on \[ A=\{1,2,3,\ldots,14\} \] is reflexive, symmetric and transitive.

उत्तर -

Given:

\[ A=\{1,2,3,\ldots,14\} \]

\[ R=\{(x,y):3x-y=0\} \]

Reflexive:

\[ \text{For } (x,x)\in R \]

\[ \Rightarrow 3x-x=0 \]

\[ \Rightarrow 2x=0 \]

\[ \Rightarrow x=0 \]

\[ 0\notin A \]

Hence \(R\) is not Reflexive.

Symmetric:

\[ \text{If } (x,y)\in R \]

\[ \Rightarrow 3x-y=0 \]

\[ \Rightarrow y=3x \]

\[ \Rightarrow x\neq 3y \]

\[ \Rightarrow (y,x)\notin R \]

Hence \(R\) is not Symmetric.

Transitive:

\[ \text{If } (x,y)\in R \text{ and } (y,z)\in R \]

\[ \Rightarrow y=3x \text{ and } z=3y \]

\[ \Rightarrow z=9x \]

\[ z\neq 3x \]

\[ \Rightarrow (x,z)\notin R \]

Hence \(R\) is not Transitive.

\[ \therefore R \text{ is neither Reflexive, nor Symmetric, nor Transitive.} \]

Q.1 (iii) Determine whether the relation \[ R=\{(x,y): y \text{ is divisible by } x\} \] defined on the set \[ A=\{1,2,3,4,5,6\} \] is reflexive, symmetric and transitive.

उत्तर -

Given:

\[ A=\{1,2,3,4,5,6\} \]

\[ R=\{(x,y): x\mid y\} \]

Reflexive:

For any \(x\in A\)

Since every number divides itself.

\[ x\mid x \]

\[ \Rightarrow (x,x)\in R \]

Hence \(R\) is Reflexive.

Symmetric:

\[ \text{If } (x,y)\in R \]

\[ \Rightarrow x\mid y \]

\[ 2\mid 4 \]

\[ 4\nmid 2 \]

\[ \Rightarrow (y,x)\notin R \]

Hence \(R\) is not Symmetric.

Transitive:

\[ \text{If } (x,y)\in R \text{ and } (y,z)\in R \]

\[ \Rightarrow x\mid y \text{ and } y\mid z \]

\[ \Rightarrow x\mid z \]

\[ \Rightarrow (x,z)\in R \]

Hence \(R\) is Transitive.

\[ \therefore R \text{ is Reflexive and Transitive but not Symmetric.} \]

Q.1 (iv) Determine whether the relation \[ R=\{(x,y):x-y \text{ is an integer}\} \] defined on the set \(Z\) of all integers is reflexive, symmetric and transitive.

उत्तर -

Given:

\[ R=\{(x,y):x-y\in Z\} \]

Reflexive:

For any \(x\in Z\)

Since \(0\) is an integer.

\[ x-x=0 \]

\[ \Rightarrow x-x\in Z \]

\[ \Rightarrow (x,x)\in R \]

Hence \(R\) is Reflexive.

Symmetric:

\[ \text{If } (x,y)\in R \]

\[ \Rightarrow x-y\in Z \]

\[ \Rightarrow -(x-y)\in Z \]

\[ \Rightarrow y-x\in Z \]

\[ \Rightarrow (y,x)\in R \]

Hence \(R\) is Symmetric.

Transitive:

\[ \text{If } (x,y)\in R \text{ and } (y,z)\in R \]

\[ \Rightarrow x-y\in Z \text{ and } y-z\in Z \]

\[ \Rightarrow (x-y)+(y-z)\in Z \]

\[ \Rightarrow x-z\in Z \]

\[ \Rightarrow (x,z)\in R \]

Hence \(R\) is Transitive.

\[ \therefore R \text{ is Reflexive, Symmetric and Transitive.} \]

Q.2 Show that the relation \(R\) in the set \(R\) of real numbers, defined as \[ R=\{(a,b):a\le b^2\} \] is neither reflexive nor symmetric nor transitive.

उत्तर -

Given:

\[ R=\{(a,b):a\le b^2\} \]

Reflexive:

\[ \text{For } (a,a)\in R \]

\[ \Rightarrow a\le a^2 \]

\[ a=\frac12 \]

\[ \Rightarrow \frac12 \nleq \frac14 \]

\[ \Rightarrow \left(\frac12,\frac12\right)\notin R \]

Hence \(R\) is not Reflexive.

Symmetric:

\[ \text{If } (a,b)\in R \]

\[ \Rightarrow a\le b^2 \]

\[ b\le a^2 \text{ does not follow} \]

\[ \Rightarrow (b,a)\notin R \]

Hence \(R\) is not Symmetric.

Transitive:

\[ \text{If } (a,b)\in R \text{ and } (b,c)\in R \]

\[ \Rightarrow a\le b^2 \text{ and } b\le c^2 \]

\[ a\le c^2 \text{ does not follow} \]

\[ \Rightarrow (a,c)\notin R \]

Hence \(R\) is not Transitive.

\[ \therefore R \text{ is neither Reflexive, nor Symmetric, nor Transitive.} \]

Q.12 Show that the relation \[ R=\{(T_1,T_2):T_1 \text{ is similar to } T_2\} \] defined in the set \(A\) of all triangles is an equivalence relation. Consider three right angled triangles \(T_1\) with sides 3, 4, 5; \(T_2\) with sides 5, 12, 13 and \(T_3\) with sides 6, 8, 10. Which triangles among \(T_1, T_2\) and \(T_3\) are related?

उत्तर -

Given:

\[ A=\text{Set of all triangles} \]

\[ R=\{(T_1,T_2):T_1 \sim T_2\} \]

Reflexive:

For \(T \in A\)

\[ \Rightarrow T \sim T \]

\[ \Rightarrow (T,T)\in R \]

Hence \(R\) is Reflexive.

Symmetric:

For \(T_1,T_2 \in A\)

When \((T_1,T_2)\in R\)

\[ \Rightarrow T_1 \sim T_2 \]

\[ \Rightarrow T_2 \sim T_1 \]

\[ \Rightarrow (T_2,T_1)\in R \]

Hence \(R\) is Symmetric.

Transitive:

For \(T_1,T_2,T_3 \in A\)

When \((T_1,T_2)\in R\) and \((T_2,T_3)\in R\)

\[ \Rightarrow T_1 \sim T_2 \]

\[ \text{and } T_2 \sim T_3 \]

\[ \Rightarrow T_1 \sim T_3 \]

\[ \Rightarrow (T_1,T_3)\in R \]

Hence \(R\) is Transitive.

Therefore \(R\) is an Equivalence Relation.

Related Triangles:

\[ T_1=(3,4,5) \]

\[ T_3=(6,8,10) \]

\[ \frac{6}{3}=\frac{8}{4}=\frac{10}{5}=2 \]

\[ \Rightarrow T_1 \sim T_3 \]

For \(T_2=(5,12,13)\),

\[ \frac{5}{3}\neq\frac{12}{4}\neq\frac{13}{5} \]

\[ \Rightarrow T_2 \not\sim T_1 \]

\[ \Rightarrow T_2 \not\sim T_3 \]

Hence only \(T_1\) and \(T_3\) are related.

Q.1 (iii) Determine whether the relation \[ R=\{(x,y): y \text{ is divisible by } x\} \] in the set \[ A=\{1,2,3,4,5,6\} \] is reflexive, symmetric and transitive.

उत्तर -

Reflexive:

सभी \(x \in A\) के लिए \(x\), \(x\) से विभाज्य है।

\[ (x,x)\in R \]

अतः \(R\) Reflexive है।

Symmetric:

यदि \((2,4)\in R\), क्योंकि 4, 2 से विभाज्य है।

परन्तु 2, 4 से विभाज्य नहीं है।

\[ (4,2)\notin R \]

अतः \(R\) Symmetric नहीं है।

Transitive:

यदि \((x,y)\in R\) तथा \((y,z)\in R\),

तो \(y\), \(x\) से विभाज्य है तथा \(z\), \(y\) से विभाज्य है।

अतः \(z\), \(x\) से भी विभाज्य होगा।

\[ (x,z)\in R \]

अतः \(R\) Transitive है।

निष्कर्ष : \(R\) Reflexive तथा Transitive है, किन्तु Symmetric नहीं है।

Q.12 Show that the relation \[ R=\{(T_1,T_2): T_1 \text{ is similar to } T_2\} \] defined on the set \(A\) of all triangles is an equivalence relation.

उत्तर -

Given:

\[ A=\text{Set of all triangles} \]

\[ R=\{(T_1,T_2): T_1 \sim T_2\} \]

Reflexive:

For any triangle \(T \in A\)

Since every triangle is similar to itself.

\[ T \sim T \]

\[ \Longrightarrow (T,T)\in R \]

Hence \(R\) is Reflexive.

Symmetric:

Let \(T_1,T_2 \in A\).

\[ \text{If } (T_1,T_2)\in R \]

\[ \Longrightarrow T_1 \sim T_2 \]

\[ \Longrightarrow T_2 \sim T_1 \]

\[ \Longrightarrow (T_2,T_1)\in R \]

Hence \(R\) is Symmetric.

Transitive:

Let \(T_1,T_2,T_3 \in A\).

\[ \text{If } (T_1,T_2)\in R \text{ and } (T_2,T_3)\in R \]

\[ \Longrightarrow T_1 \sim T_2 \text{ and } T_2 \sim T_3 \]

\[ \Longrightarrow T_1 \sim T_3 \]

\[ \Longrightarrow (T_1,T_3)\in R \]

Hence \(R\) is Transitive.

Therefore \(R\) is Reflexive, Symmetric and Transitive.

\[ \therefore R \text{ is an Equivalence Relation.} \]

\[ \Rightarrow P_2 \sim P_1 \]

Transitive:

\[ \text{If } (P_1,P_2)\in R \text{ and } (P_2,P_3)\in R \]

\[ \Rightarrow P_1 \sim P_3 \]
