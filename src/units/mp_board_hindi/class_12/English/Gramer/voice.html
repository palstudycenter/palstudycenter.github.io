📘 Voice
Rules for Changing Active Voice into Passive Voice

Rule 1. Active Voice का Object, Passive Voice का Subject बन जाता है तथा Subject, by के बाद लिखा जाता है।

Rule 2. Passive Voice में मुख्य क्रिया (Verb) का Third Form (V³) प्रयोग किया जाता है।

Rule 3. Tense के अनुसार is, am, are, was, were, be, being अथवा been का प्रयोग किया जाता है।

Rule 1 : यदि Subject के साथ Verb का First Form (V¹) हो

Formula :

Active : Subject + Verb¹ + Object

Passive : Object + is/am/are + Verb³ + by + Subject

Examples :

Active : She writes a letter.

Passive : A letter is written by her.

Active : They play cricket.

Passive : Cricket is played by them.

Rule 2 : यदि Subject के साथ Verb का Second Form (V²) हो

Formula :

Active : Subject + Verb² + Object

Passive : Object + was/were + Verb³ + by + Subject

Examples :

Active : The teacher praised the student.

Passive : The student was praised by the teacher.

Active : They completed the work.

Passive : The work was completed by them.

Rule 3 : यदि Will/Shall के साथ Verb का First Form (V¹) हो

Formula :

Active : Subject + will/shall + Verb¹ + Object

Passive : Object + will/shall + be + Verb³ + by + Subject

Examples :

Active : They will complete the work.

Passive : The work will be completed by them.

Active : We shall help him.

Passive : He will be helped by us.

Rule 4 : यदि Verb के साथ ing हो

Formula :

Active : Subject + is/am/are + Verb¹+ing + Object

Passive : Object + is/am/are + being + Verb³ + by + Subject

Examples :

Active : The students are reading the book.

Passive : The book is being read by the students.

Active : She is preparing food.

Passive : Food is being prepared by her.

Rule 5 : यदि Subject के साथ Has / Have / Had हो

Formula :

Active : Subject + has/have/had + Verb³ + Object

Passive : Object + has/have/had + been + Verb³ + by + Subject

Examples :

Active : She has written a letter.

Passive : A letter has been written by her.

Active : They had finished the work.

Passive : The work had been finished by them.

Special Rule 1 : Imperative Sentences (Let Rule)

जिन वाक्यों में Subject नहीं होता तथा वाक्य Verb से प्रारम्भ होता है, उनके Passive Voice में Let का प्रयोग किया जाता है।

Formula :

Active : Verb + Object

Passive : Let + Object + be + Verb³

Examples :

Active : Open the door.

Passive : Let the door be opened.

Active : Post the letter.

Passive : Let the letter be posted.

Active : Do it.

Passive : Let it be done.

Special Rule 2 : Modal Auxiliaries

Modal Auxiliary (can, could, may, might, should, must आदि) वाले वाक्यों में Modal के बाद be लगाया जाता है।

Formula :

Modal + be + Verb³

Examples :

Active : You can solve this problem.

Passive : This problem can be solved by you.

Active : We should obey the rules.

Passive : The rules should be obeyed by us.

Special Rule 3 : Somebody / Everybody / People

Somebody, Everybody, People आदि से प्रारम्भ होने वाले वाक्यों के Passive Voice में सामान्यतः by + Subject नहीं लिखा जाता।

Examples :

Active : Somebody cleans the room every day.

Passive : The room is cleaned every day.

Active : People speak English all over the world.

Passive : English is spoken all over the world.

Special Rule 4 : Sentences Beginning with Who

Who से प्रारम्भ होने वाले वाक्यों का Passive Voice सामान्यतः By whom से प्रारम्भ होता है।

Example :

Active : Who wrote this poem?

Passive : By whom was this poem written?

Special Rule 5 : Interrogative Sentences

What, When, Where, Why, How आदि Question Words Passive Voice में भी यथावत रहते हैं।

Examples :

Active : What did you buy?

Passive : What was bought by you?

Active : Where did he keep the books?

Passive : Where were the books kept by him?

Active : When did they complete the work?

Passive : When was the work completed by them?

Special Rule 6 : Passive Voice of Negative Sentences

Negative Sentences में not को Auxiliary Verb के बाद लिखा जाता है।

Example :

Active : They do not play hockey.

Passive : Hockey is not played by them.

Active : She did not write a letter.

Passive : A letter was not written by her.

Special Rule 7 : Passive Voice of Interrogative Sentences

प्रश्नवाचक वाक्यों में Auxiliary Verb को Subject से पहले रखा जाता है।

Examples :

Active : Did he write a letter?

Passive : Was a letter written by him?

Active : Has she completed the work?

Passive : Has the work been completed by her?

Special Rule 8 : Passive Voice of Double Object Sentences

कुछ वाक्यों में दो Object होते हैं। ऐसे वाक्यों में किसी भी Object को Subject बनाया जा सकता है।

Example :

Active : The teacher gave me a prize.

Passive : I was given a prize by the teacher.

or

A prize was given to me by the teacher.

Special Rule 9 : Passive Voice of Imperative Sentences with Kindly / Please

Examples :

Active : Please help me.

Passive : You are requested to help me.

Active : Kindly open the window.

Passive : You are requested to open the window.

Important Examination Questions

1. Change the Voice : The teacher praised the student.

Answer : The student was praised by the teacher.

2. Change the Voice : They will complete the work tomorrow.

Answer : The work will be completed by them tomorrow.

3. Change the Voice : Who wrote this letter?

Answer : By whom was this letter written?

4. Change the Voice : Open the door.

Answer : Let the door be opened.
