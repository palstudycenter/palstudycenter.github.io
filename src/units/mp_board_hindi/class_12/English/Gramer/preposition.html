📘 Preposition
Rule 1 : Use of At

At का प्रयोग निश्चित समय, स्थान या बिंदु (Point) के लिए किया जाता है।

Examples :

The train arrived at 8 o'clock.

He lives at Gwalior.

She was standing at the bus stop.

Rule 2 : Use of In

In का प्रयोग बड़े स्थान, महीनों, वर्षों तथा अवधि (Period of Time) के लिए किया जाता है।

Examples :

I was born in 2008.

The examination will be held in March.

She lives in India.

Rule 3 : Use of On

On का प्रयोग दिन, तारीख तथा सतह (Surface) के लिए किया जाता है।

Examples :

The school is closed on Sunday.

My birthday is on 15 August.

The book is on the table.

Rule 4 : Use of Since and For

Since का प्रयोग निश्चित समय (Point of Time) के लिए किया जाता है।

For का प्रयोग समय अवधि (Period of Time) के लिए किया जाता है।

Examples :

He has been ill since Monday.

She has lived here for ten years.

Rule 5 : Use of By

By का अर्थ "तक", "द्वारा" या "पास में" होता है।

Examples :

The work will be completed by evening.

The letter was written by Mohan.

He sat by me.

Rule 6 : Use of Between and Among

Between का प्रयोग दो व्यक्तियों या वस्तुओं के लिए होता है।

Among का प्रयोग दो से अधिक व्यक्तियों या वस्तुओं के लिए होता है।

Examples :

The match was played between India and Australia.

He distributed sweets among the students.

Rule 7 : Use of Into and In

Into गति (Movement) को दर्शाता है।

In स्थिति (Position) को दर्शाता है।

Examples :

The boy jumped into the river.

The fish is in the water.

Rule 8 : Use of Beside and Besides

Beside = बगल में

Besides = इसके अतिरिक्त

Examples :

Ram sat beside Shyam.

Besides English, he knows Hindi.

Rule 9 : Use of From

From का प्रयोग स्रोत, प्रारम्भिक स्थान या आरंभिक समय के लिए किया जाता है।

Examples :

He came from Delhi.

The shop remains open from 9 a.m.

Important Board Examination Examples

1. He has been living here _____ 2018.

Answer : since

2. The book is _____ the table.

Answer : on

3. She was born _____ January.

Answer : in

4. The train arrived _____ 7 p.m.

Answer : at

5. The teacher divided the sweets _____ the students.

Answer : among
