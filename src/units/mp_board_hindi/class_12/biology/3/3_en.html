What is amniocentesis?

Amniocentesis is a technique of foetal testing generally carried out between the 15th and 20th week of pregnancy.

In this method, with the help of ultrasound, a small sample of amniotic fluid is withdrawn from the uterus using a thin needle.

The foetal cells present in the amniotic fluid are cultured and their chromosomes are examined, by which genetic and chromosomal abnormalities occurring in the foetus are detected.

  1. It is helpful in identifying sex-linked genetic diseases present in the foetus, so that an appropriate medical decision can be taken in time.

Why has a legal ban been imposed on amniocentesis?

In the present time, this technique of foetal testing has been misused.

Through it, sex determination was carried out leading to female foeticide, due to which the male-female ratio in society began to become imbalanced.

For this very reason, a legal ban has been imposed in India on the use of amniocentesis for sex determination.

What are the natural methods of birth control?

This process works on the principle of preventing the meeting of the egg and the sperm. In this, fertilisation is prevented.

This is done in the following ways.

  1. Withdrawal method (coitus interruptus):- In this process, during coitus, the male withdraws the penis from the female's body before ejaculation of semen.
  2. Periodic abstinence:- In this, a couple does not have coitus between the 10th and 17th day of the menstrual cycle because there are very high chances of conception during this period.
  3. Lactational amenorrhoea:- As long as the mother breastfeeds the infant after childbirth, the menstrual cycle or ovulation does not begin.

This method is considered effective for about 6 months after delivery.

What is meant by barrier methods of birth control?

In this method, a physical barrier is used to prevent the male and female gametes from meeting.

Such as:-

  1. Condom
  2. Cervical cap
  3. Contraceptive diaphragm
What is meant by intrauterine devices for birth control?

Answer:
Intrauterine devices (IUDs) for birth control are contraceptive devices that are placed inside a woman's uterus in order to prevent pregnancy.

These devices prevent pregnancy by preventing fertilisation or implantation.

These devices are effective and safe for a long period and are generally inserted by a doctor.

Example - Copper-T

How does the intrauterine device (IUD) reduce fertility? (2025)

Answer:

The intrauterine device (IUD) reduces fertility in the following ways—

  1. The IUD produces a local inflammatory response in the uterus, which reduces the activity of sperm.
  2. Copper ions released from copper-containing IUDs such as Copper-T are harmful to sperm, causing them to be destroyed.
  3. It makes the inner lining of the uterus (endometrium) such that the fertilised egg is unable to implant.

In this way, by preventing both fertilisation and implantation, the IUD reduces the chances of conception.

Write the full form of IUD.

Intra-Uterine Device

— The use of Copper-T cannot protect against sexually transmitted diseases, why?

Copper-T is an intrauterine contraceptive device (IUD), which is used only to prevent pregnancy.

The use of Copper-T cannot protect against sexually transmitted diseases because—

  1. Copper-T is placed inside the uterus.
  2. It does not prevent pathogens from entering the body during sexual contact.
  3. It has no kind of barrier (protection) in it.

What is meant by sterilisation or surgical method? How many types are there?

Answer:
Sterilisation or the surgical method is a permanent contraceptive measure in which the reproductive tubes of a woman or man are cut or tied through surgery (operation), making fertilisation impossible.

This method is permanent, so it is adopted after the family is complete.

It is of two types.

  1. Vasectomy
  2. Tubectomy
What is vasectomy (excision of the vas deferens)?

(M.P. 2009)

Vasectomy is a permanent sterilisation surgery performed on men.

In this method, the man's vas deferens is cut or tied and closed. Because of this, sperm are unable to reach the semen, so fertilisation does not take place and conception is prevented.

What is tubectomy (excision of the tubes)?

Tubectomy is a permanent sterilisation surgery performed on women.

In this method, the woman's fallopian tubes are cut or tied and closed. As a result, the egg and sperm are unable to meet, so fertilisation is prevented and pregnancy does not occur.

Answer:
Medical Termination of Pregnancy (M.T.P.) is the medical procedure in which a pregnancy is safely terminated by a doctor.

This procedure is adopted when—

  1. The pregnancy is harmful to the mother's health
  2. There are serious abnormalities in the foetus
  3. There is a situation of rape or accidental pregnancy

Safe period for M.T.P.

  1. The safest time for M.T.P. is during the first trimester of pregnancy (the first 12 weeks).
  2. During this period the risk is lowest and complications are almost negligible.

What is an STD?

Diseases that are transmitted from one person to another through sexual intercourse are called sexually transmitted diseases. These are also called venereal diseases or hidden diseases.

STDs caused by bacteria

  1. Gonorrhoea: This disease is caused by bacteria. Gonorrhoea is caused by a bacterium called Neisseria gonorrhoeae.

Symptoms:- Burning sensation while urinating.

  1. Syphilis :- Syphilis is a sexually transmitted disease. Syphilis is caused by the bacterium Treponema pallidum.

Symptoms:- Sores on the genitals.

STDs caused by viruses :-

  1. AIDS :- AIDS is caused by viruses. AIDS weakens the immune system.

AIDS is caused by a virus called HIV.

Symptoms = Persistent fever, muscle pain.

  1. Hepatitis B :- This is also a virus-caused disease.

This disease is caused by a virus called HBV.

Symptoms:- Abdominal pain, nausea, etc.

  1. Genital warts :- This disease is caused by Human papillomavirus.

Symptoms - Formation of warts on the penis in men and on the labia, vagina and uterus in women.

What measures should a person adopt to avoid falling prey to sexually transmitted diseases?

Answer:

A person should adopt the following measures to avoid falling prey to sexually transmitted diseases

  1. Do not have sexual relations with an unknown person or with many people.
  2. Always use a condom during intercourse.
  3. If there is any suspicion of infection, immediately consult a qualified doctor for preliminary examination, and if the disease is detected, get complete treatment.
  4. One should have knowledge of the common symptoms of sexually transmitted diseases.

What is IVF or the test tube baby technique?

IVF is an assisted reproductive technique in which the woman's egg and the man's sperm are combined in the laboratory to bring about fertilisation.

Procedure :

  1. The woman is given follicle stimulating hormone (FSH) immediately after menstruation, due to which several eggs are produced instead of one.
  2. A small incision is made near the navel to remove the eggs, and the eggs are obtained.
  3. The egg is placed in a solution containing sperm, where fertilisation takes place and a zygote is formed.
  4. When the embryo reaches the 8–16 cell stage, it is implanted into the uterus.
Write the full form of IVF.

In Vitro Fertilisation

What is a test tube baby?

(M.P. 2017, 18, 19)

Answer:

A test tube baby is a child who is born through the IVF (in vitro fertilisation) process.

It is called a "test tube baby" because fertilisation takes place in a test tube or dish in the laboratory, not inside the body.

What is intracytoplasmic sperm injection (ICSI)?

ICSI is an assisted reproductive technique.

This method is adopted when the husband's sperm have reduced motility or the sperm are unable to fertilise the egg.

In this method, a single sperm of the husband is selected with the help of a micropipette. The selected sperm is injected directly into the cytoplasm of the woman's egg (ovum), so that fertilisation can take place.

Write the full form of ICSI.

Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection

What is gamete intrafallopian transfer (GIFT)?

Answer:

GIFT is an assisted reproductive technique used for women who do not produce eggs, but in whom fertilisation and embryonic development are possible.

In this, an egg is obtained from another donor woman. This donor egg is combined with the husband's sperm. This gamete (egg + sperm) is transferred directly into the woman's fallopian tube, so that natural fertilisation and embryonic development can take place there.

Write the full form of GIFT.

(2022/22)

Answer: Gamete Intrafallopian Transfer.

What is meant by artificial insemination?

Artificial insemination (AI) is a method used for men who are unable to deliver semen into the woman's vagina during natural intercourse, or whose semen has a deficiency of sperm.

In this method, the sperm (semen) of the husband or of a healthy donor is introduced directly into the woman's vagina or uterus, so that fertilisation and pregnancy become possible.

What is surrogacy?

Surrogacy is the process in which a woman (surrogate mother) carries a child in her womb for another woman and, after birth, hands the child over to that woman or couple.

What is a surrogate mother?

In some women, fertilisation of the egg does take place, but for some reason the development of the embryo does not occur. In such a situation—

The woman's egg and her husband's sperm are fertilised artificially.

The embryo, at the 32-cell stage, is implanted into the uterus of another willing woman.

The woman who carries the embryo in her womb and, after birth, hands it over to the real parents, is called the surrogate mother.
