📘 7(B) — Phenol
Question: What is phenol?

An organic compound in which a hydroxyl group (–OH) is directly attached to a carbon atom of the benzene ring is called phenol. Its molecular formula is C₆H₅OH. It is also called hydroxybenzene.

Question: Write the general methods of preparing phenol.

1. From benzenesulphonic acid:

Heating benzenesulphonic acid with NaOH gives sodium benzenesulphonate. Heating this with molten NaOH gives sodium phenoxide. Acidification of sodium phenoxide gives phenol.

2. From chlorobenzene — Dow process:

Heating chlorobenzene with aqueous NaOH at high temperature and pressure gives sodium phenoxide. Acidification of this gives phenol.

3. From hydrolysis of diazonium salts:

Heating benzenediazonium chloride with water gives phenol, along with the release of nitrogen gas.

4. From cumene:

Oxidation of cumene by atmospheric oxygen gives cumene hydroperoxide. Acid hydrolysis of this gives phenol and acetone.

5. From decarboxylation of sodium salicylate:

Heating sodium salicylate with soda lime (NaOH + CaO) gives phenol through decarboxylation.

6. From Grignard reagent:

Reacting phenylmagnesium bromide with oxygen gives magnesium phenoxide bromide. Hydrolysis of this gives phenol.

7. Raschig process:

In the Raschig process, heating benzene, HCl, and air in the presence of a catalyst gives chlorobenzene. Chlorobenzene is then hydrolyzed to obtain phenol.
