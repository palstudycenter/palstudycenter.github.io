Q.1 Show that the function \[ f:\mathbb{R}^{*}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^{*},\qquad f(x)=\frac1x \] is one-one and onto, where \(\mathbb{R}^{*}\) is the set of all non-zero real numbers. Is the result true if the domain \(\mathbb{R}^{*}\) is replaced by \(\mathbb{N}\) with the same co-domain \(\mathbb{R}^{*}\)?

उत्तर -

One-One (Injective):

\[\text{Let} \forall x_1 , x_2\in R\ \]

\[ \text{Now } f(x_1)=f(x_2) \]

\[ \Rightarrow \frac1{x_1}=\frac1{x_2} \]

\[ \Rightarrow x_1=x_2 \]

Hence \(f\) is One-One.

Onto (Surjective):

\[\text{Let} \forall y\in R\ \]

\[ \ \exists x\in\mathbb{R}^{*} \]

\[\ : \ f(x)=y \]

\[\Rightarrow \frac1x=y\]

\[\Rightarrow \frac1y=x\in\mathbb{R}^{*} \]

Hence \(f\) is Onto.

When Domain is \(\mathbb{N}\):

\[ f:\mathbb{N}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^{*} \]

\[ f(x)=\frac1x \]

\[ 2\in\mathbb{R}^{*} \]

\[ \nexists x\in\mathbb{N}\text{ such that }\frac1x=2 \]

Hence \(f\) is One-One but not Onto.

Q.2 (i) Check the injectivity and surjectivity of the function \[ f:\mathbb{N}\rightarrow\mathbb{N},\qquad f(x)=x^2 \]

उत्तर -

One-One (Injective):

\[ \text{Let }\forall x_1,x_2\in\mathbb N \]

\[ \text{Now }f(x_1)=f(x_2) \]

\[ \Rightarrow x_1^2=x_2^2 \]

\[ \Rightarrow x_1=x_2 \]

Hence \(f\) is One-One.

Onto (Surjective):

\[ \text{Let }\forall y\in\mathbb N \]

\[ \exists x\in\mathbb N \]

\[ :f(x)=y \]

\[ \Rightarrow x^2=y \]

\[ \Rightarrow x=\sqrt y\notin\mathbb N \]

Hence \(f\) is not Onto.

\[ \therefore f \text{ is One-One but not Onto.} \]

Q.7 (i) State whether the function \[ f:\mathbb R\rightarrow\mathbb R,\qquad f(x)=3-4x \] is One-One, Onto or Bijective.

उत्तर -

One-One (Injective):

\[ \text{Let }\forall x_1,x_2\in\mathbb R \]

\[ \text{Now }f(x_1)=f(x_2) \]

\[ \Rightarrow 3-4x_1=3-4x_2 \]

\[ \Rightarrow x_1=x_2 \]

Hence \(f\) is One-One.

Onto (Surjective):

\[ \text{Let }\forall y\in\mathbb R \]

\[ \exists x\in\mathbb R \]

\[ :f(x)=y \]

\[ \Rightarrow 3-4x=y \]

\[ \Rightarrow x=\frac{3-y}{4}\in\mathbb R \]

Hence \(f\) is Onto.

\[ \therefore f \text{ is Bijective.} \]

Q.8 Let \(A\) and \(B\) be sets. Show that the function \[ f:A\times B\rightarrow B\times A \] defined by \[ f(a,b)=(b,a) \] is Bijective.

उत्तर -

One-One (Injective):

\[ \text{Let }\forall (a_1,b_1),(a_2,b_2)\in A\times B \]

\[ \text{Now }f(a_1,b_1)=f(a_2,b_2) \]

\[ \Rightarrow (b_1,a_1)=(b_2,a_2) \]

\[ \Rightarrow a_1=a_2,\ b_1=b_2 \]

\[ \Rightarrow (a_1,b_1)=(a_2,b_2) \]

Hence \(f\) is One-One.

Onto (Surjective):

\[ \text{Let }\forall (b,a)\in B\times A \]

\[ \exists (a,b)\in A\times B \]

\[ :f(a,b)=(b,a) \]

Hence \(f\) is Onto.

\[ \therefore f \text{ is Bijective.} \]

Q.9 Let \[ f:\mathbb N\rightarrow\mathbb N \] be defined by \[ f(n)= \begin{cases} \dfrac{n+1}{2},&\text{if }n\text{ is odd}\\[2mm] \dfrac n2,&\text{if }n\text{ is even} \end{cases} \] State whether the function \(f\) is Bijective. Justify your answer.

उत्तर -

One-One (Injective):

\[ f(1)=1=f(2) \]

\[ \Rightarrow 1\ne2 \]

Hence \(f\) is not One-One.

Onto (Surjective):

\[ \text{Let }\forall y\in\mathbb N \]

\[ \exists x=2y\in\mathbb N \]

\[ :f(x)=\frac{2y}{2}=y \]

Hence \(f\) is Onto.

\[ \therefore f \text{ is not Bijective.} \]

Q.10 Let \[ A=\mathbb{R}-\{3\},\qquad B=\mathbb{R}-\{1\} \] Consider the function \[ f:A\rightarrow B,\qquad f(x)=\frac{x-2}{x-3} \] Is \(f\) One-One and Onto? Justify your answer.

उत्तर -

One-One (Injective):

\[ \text{Let }\forall x_1,x_2\in A \]

\[ \text{Now }f(x_1)=f(x_2) \]

\[ \Rightarrow \frac{x_1-2}{x_1-3} = \frac{x_2-2}{x_2-3} \]

\[ \Rightarrow (x_1-2)(x_2-3) = (x_2-2)(x_1-3) \]

\[ \Rightarrow x_1=x_2 \]

Hence \(f\) is One-One.

Onto (Surjective):

\[ \text{Let }\forall y\in B \]

\[ \exists x\in A \]

\[ :f(x)=y \]

\[ \Rightarrow \frac{x-2}{x-3}=y \]

\[ \Rightarrow x=\frac{3y-2}{y-1}\in A \]

Hence \(f\) is Onto.

\[ \therefore f \text{ is Bijective.} \]
