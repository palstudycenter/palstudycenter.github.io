Q.1 Prove that \[ 3\sin^{-1}(x)=\sin^{-1}(3x-4x^3) \]

उत्तर -

Let

\[ x=\sin\theta \]

\[ \theta\ =\sin^{-1}(x) \]

Now

\[ \sin^{-1}(3x-4x^3) \]

\[ =\sin^{-1}(3\sin\theta-4\sin^3\theta) \]

\[ =\sin^{-1}(\sin3\theta) \]

\[ =3\theta \]

\[ =3\sin^{-1}x \]

Q.2 Prove that \[ 3\cos^{-1}(x)=\cos^{-1}(4x^3-3x) \]

उत्तर -

Let

\[ x=\cos\theta \]

\[ \theta=\cos^{-1}(x) \]

Now

\[ \cos^{-1}(4x^3-3x) \]

\[ =\cos^{-1}(4\cos^3\theta-3\cos\theta) \]

\[ =\cos^{-1}(\cos3\theta) \]

\[ =3\theta \]

\[ =3\cos^{-1}(x) \]

Q.3 Find the value of \[ \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{\sqrt{1+x^2}-1}{x}\right),\qquad x\ne0 \]

उत्तर -

Let

\[ \theta=\tan^{-1}(x) \]

\[ x=\tan\theta \]

Now

\[ \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{\sqrt{1+x^2}-1}{x}\right) \]

\[ =\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{\sqrt{1+\tan^2\theta}-1}{\tan\theta}\right) \]

\[ =\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{\sec\theta-1}{\tan\theta}\right) \]

\[ =\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{1-\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}\right) \]

\[ =\tan^{-1}\left(\tan\frac{\theta}{2}\right) \]

\[ =\frac{\theta}{2} \]

\[ =\frac12\tan^{-1}(x) \]

Q.5 Prove that \[ \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{\cos x-\sin x}{\cos x+\sin x}\right) =\frac{\pi}{4}-x \]

उत्तर -

Now

\[ \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{\cos x-\sin x}{\cos x+\sin x}\right) \]

\[ =\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{1-\tan x}{1+\tan x}\right) \]

\[ =\tan^{-1}\left(\tan\left(\frac{\pi}{4}-x\right)\right) \]

\[ =\frac{\pi}{4}-x \]

Q.6 Solve \[ \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{x}{\sqrt{a^2-x^2}}\right) \]

उत्तर -

Let

\[ x=a\sin\theta \]

\[ \theta=\sin^{-1}\left(\frac{x}{a}\right) \]

Now

\[ \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{x}{\sqrt{a^2-x^2}}\right) \]

\[ =\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{a\sin\theta} {\sqrt{a^2-a^2\sin^2\theta}}\right) \]

\[ =\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{a\sin\theta} {a\cos\theta}\right) \]

\[ =\tan^{-1}(\tan\theta) \]

\[ =\theta \]

\[ =\sin^{-1}\left(\frac{x}{a}\right) \]

Q.7 Solve \[ \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{3a^2x-x^3}{a^3-3ax^2}\right) \]

उत्तर -

Let

\[ x=a\tan\theta \]

\[ \theta=\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{x}{a}\right) \]

Now

\[ \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{3a^2x-x^3}{a^3-3ax^2}\right) \]

\[ =\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{3\tan\theta-\tan^3\theta} {1-3\tan^2\theta}\right) \]

\[ =\tan^{-1}(\tan3\theta) \]

\[ =3\theta \]

\[ =3\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{x}{a}\right) \]

Q.8 Solve \[ \tan^{-1}\left[2\cos\left(2\sin^{-1}\frac{1}{2}\right)\right] \]

उत्तर -

Now

\[ \tan^{-1}\left[2\cos\left(2\sin^{-1}\frac{1}{2}\right)\right] \]

\[ =\tan^{-1}\left(2\cos\left(2\times\frac{\pi}{6}\right)\right) \]

\[ =\tan^{-1}\left(2\cos\frac{\pi}{3}\right) \]

\[ =\tan^{-1}\left(2\times\frac12\right) \]

\[ =\tan^{-1}(1) \]

\[ =\frac{\pi}{4} \]

Q.9 Solve \[ \tan\frac{1}{2}\left[\sin^{-1}\left(\frac{2x}{1+x^2}\right)+\cos^{-1}\left(\frac{1-y^2}{1+y^2}\right)\right] \]

उत्तर -

Now

\[ \tan\frac{1}{2}\left[\sin^{-1}\left(\frac{2x}{1+x^2}\right)+\cos^{-1}\left(\frac{1-y^2}{1+y^2}\right)\right] \]

\[ =\tan\frac{1}{2}\left[2\tan^{-1}x+2\tan^{-1}y\right] \]

\[ =\tan\left(\tan^{-1}x+\tan^{-1}y\right) \]

\[ =\frac{x+y}{1-xy} \]

Q.10 Find the value of \[ \sin^{-1}\left(\sin\frac{2\pi}{3}\right) \]

उत्तर -

\[ \sin^{-1}\left(\sin\frac{2\pi}{3}\right) \]

\[ =\sin^{-1}\left(\sin\left(\pi-\frac{\pi}{3}\right)\right) \]

\[ =\sin^{-1}\left(\sin\frac{\pi}{3}\right) \]

\[ =\frac{\pi}{3} \]

Q.11 Find the value of \[ \tan^{-1}\left(\tan\frac{3\pi}{4}\right) \]

उत्तर -

\[ \tan^{-1}\left(\tan\frac{3\pi}{4}\right) \]

\[ =\tan^{-1}\left(\tan\left(\pi-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\right) \]

\[ =\tan^{-1}\left(-\tan\frac{\pi}{4}\right) \]

\[ =\tan^{-1}(-1) \]

\[ =-\frac{\pi}{4} \]

Q.12 Find the value of \[ \tan\left(\sin^{-1}\frac35+\cot^{-1}\frac32\right) \]

उत्तर -

\[ \tan\left(\sin^{-1}\frac35+\cot^{-1}\frac32\right) \]

\[ =\tan\left(\tan^{-1}\frac34+\tan^{-1}\frac23\right) \]

\[ =\tan\left(\tan^{-1}\left( \frac{\frac34+\frac23} {1-\frac34\cdot\frac23} \right)\right) \]

\[ = \frac{\frac34+\frac23} {1-\left(\frac34\right)\left(\frac23\right)} \]

\[ = \frac{\frac{9+8}{12}} {1-\frac12} \]

\[ = \frac{\frac{17}{12}} {\frac12} \]

\[ = \frac{17}{12}\times2 \]

\[ =\frac{17}{6} \]
