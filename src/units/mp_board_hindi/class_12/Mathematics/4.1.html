Q.1 Evaluate the determinant \[ \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 4\\ -5 & -1 \end{vmatrix} \]

उत्तर -

\[ \Delta= \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 4\\ -5 & -1 \end{vmatrix} \]

\[ =(2)(-1)-(4)(-5) \]

\[ =-2-(-20) \]

\[ =-2+20 \]

\[ =18 \]

Q.2 (i) Find the value of \[ \begin{vmatrix} \cos\theta & -\sin\theta\\ \sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{vmatrix} \]

उत्तर -

\[ \Delta= \begin{vmatrix} \cos\theta & -\sin\theta\\ \sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{vmatrix} \]

\[ =(\cos\theta)(\cos\theta)-(-\sin\theta)(\sin\theta) \]

\[ =\cos^2\theta+\sin^2\theta \]

\[ =1 \]

Q.2 (ii) Find the value of \[ \begin{vmatrix} x^2-x+1 & x-1\\ x+1 & x+1 \end{vmatrix} \]

उत्तर -

\[ \Delta= \begin{vmatrix} x^2-x+1 & x-1\\ x+1 & x+1 \end{vmatrix} \]

\[ =(x^2-x+1)(x+1)-(x-1)(x+1) \]

\[ =(x+1)\left[(x^2-x+1)-(x-1)\right] \]

\[ =(x+1)(x^2-2x+2) \]

\[ =x^3-x^2+2 \]

Q.3 If \[ A= \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2\\ 4 & 2 \end{bmatrix} \] then show that \[ |2A|=4|A| \]

उत्तर -

\[ |A|= \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2\\ 4 & 2 \end{vmatrix} \]

\[ =(1)(2)-(2)(4) \]

\[ =2-8 \]

\[ =-6 \]

Now

\[ 2A= \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 4\\ 8 & 4 \end{bmatrix} \]

Now

\[ |2A|= \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 4\\ 8 & 4 \end{vmatrix} \]

\[ =(2)(4)-(4)(8) \]

\[ =8-32 \]

\[ =-24 \]

\[ =4(-6) \]

\[ =4|A| \]

Q.4 If \[ A= \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 1\\ 0 & 1 & 2\\ 0 & 0 & 4 \end{bmatrix} \] then show that \[ |3A|=27|A| \]

उत्तर -

\[ |A|= \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 0 & 1\\ 0 & 1 & 2\\ 0 & 0 & 4 \end{vmatrix} \]

\[ =(1)(1)(4) \]

\[ =4 \]

Now

\[ 3A= \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 0 & 3\\ 0 & 3 & 6\\ 0 & 0 & 12 \end{bmatrix} \]

\[ |3A|= \begin{vmatrix} 3 & 0 & 3\\ 0 & 3 & 6\\ 0 & 0 & 12 \end{vmatrix} \]

\[ =(3)(3)(12) \]

\[ =108 \]

\[ =27\times4 \]

\[ =27|A| \]

\[ \therefore |3A|=27|A| \]

Q.5 (i) Evaluate \[ \begin{vmatrix} 3 & -1 & -2\\ 0 & 0 & -1\\ 3 & -5 & 0 \end{vmatrix} \]

उत्तर -

\[ \Delta= \begin{vmatrix} 3 & -1 & -2\\ 0 & 0 & -1\\ 3 & -5 & 0 \end{vmatrix} \]

\[ =3 \begin{vmatrix} 0 & -1\\ -5 & 0 \end{vmatrix} -(-1) \begin{vmatrix} 0 & -1\\ 3 & 0 \end{vmatrix} +(-2) \begin{vmatrix} 0 & 0\\ 3 & -5 \end{vmatrix} \]

\[ =3[(0)(0)-(-1)(-5)] +[(0)(0)-(-1)(3)] +(-2)[(0)(-5)-(0)(3)] \]

\[ =3(0-5)+(0+3)+(-2)(0) \]

\[ =-15+3 \]

\[ =-12 \]

Q.5 (ii) Evaluate \[ \begin{vmatrix} 3 & -4 & 5\\ 1 & 1 & -2\\ 2 & 3 & 1 \end{vmatrix} \]

उत्तर -

\[ \Delta= \begin{vmatrix} 3 & -4 & 5\\ 1 & 1 & -2\\ 2 & 3 & 1 \end{vmatrix} \]

\[ =3 \begin{vmatrix} 1 & -2\\ 3 & 1 \end{vmatrix} -(-4) \begin{vmatrix} 1 & -2\\ 2 & 1 \end{vmatrix} +5 \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1\\ 2 & 3 \end{vmatrix} \]

\[ =3[(1)(1)-(-2)(3)] +4[(1)(1)-(-2)(2)] +5[(1)(3)-(1)(2)] \]

\[ =3(1+6)+4(1+4)+5(3-2) \]

\[ =21+20+5 \]

\[ =46 \]

Q.5 (iii) Evaluate \[ \begin{vmatrix} 0 & 1 & 2\\ -1 & 0 & -3\\ -2 & 3 & 0 \end{vmatrix} \]

उत्तर -

\[ \Delta= \begin{vmatrix} 0 & 1 & 2\\ -1 & 0 & -3\\ -2 & 3 & 0 \end{vmatrix} \]

\[ = 0 \begin{vmatrix} 0 & -3\\ 3 & 0 \end{vmatrix} -1 \begin{vmatrix} -1 & -3\\ -2 & 0 \end{vmatrix} +2 \begin{vmatrix} -1 & 0\\ -2 & 3 \end{vmatrix} \]

\[ = 0 -\left[(-1)(0)-(-3)(-2)\right] +2\left[(-1)(3)-(0)(-2)\right] \]

\[ = 0-(0-6)+2(-3) \]

\[ = 6-6 \]

\[ =0 \]

\[ \therefore \begin{vmatrix} 0 & 1 & 2\\ -1 & 0 & -3\\ -2 & 3 & 0 \end{vmatrix} =0 \]

Q.5 (iv) Evaluate \[ \begin{vmatrix} 2 & -1 & -2\\ 0 & 2 & -1\\ 3 & -5 & 0 \end{vmatrix} \]

उत्तर -

\[ \Delta= \begin{vmatrix} 2 & -1 & -2\\ 0 & 2 & -1\\ 3 & -5 & 0 \end{vmatrix} \]

\[ = 2 \begin{vmatrix} 2 & -1\\ -5 & 0 \end{vmatrix} -(-1) \begin{vmatrix} 0 & -1\\ 3 & 0 \end{vmatrix} +(-2) \begin{vmatrix} 0 & 2\\ 3 & -5 \end{vmatrix} \]

\[ = 2\left[(2)(0)-(-1)(-5)\right] +\left[(0)(0)-(-1)(3)\right] +(-2)\left[(0)(-5)-(2)(3)\right] \]

\[ = 2(0-5)+(0+3)+(-2)(-6) \]

\[ = -10+3+12 \]

\[ =5 \]

\[ \therefore \begin{vmatrix} 2 & -1 & -2\\ 0 & 2 & -1\\ 3 & -5 & 0 \end{vmatrix} =5 \]

Q.6 If \[ A= \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 & -2\\ 2 & 1 & -3\\ 5 & 4 & -9 \end{bmatrix} \] find \(|A|\)

उत्तर -

\[ |A|= \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 & -2\\ 2 & 1 & -3\\ 5 & 4 & -9 \end{vmatrix} \]

\[ =1 \begin{vmatrix} 1 & -3\\ 4 & -9 \end{vmatrix} -1 \begin{vmatrix} 2 & -3\\ 5 & -9 \end{vmatrix} +(-2) \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 1\\ 5 & 4 \end{vmatrix} \]

\[ =1[(1)(-9)-(-3)(4)] -[(2)(-9)-(-3)(5)] +(-2)[(2)(4)-(1)(5)] \]

\[ =(-9+12)-(-18+15)+(-2)(8-5) \]

\[ =3-(-3)-6 \]

\[ =3+3-6 \]

\[ =0 \]

\[ \therefore |A|=0 \]

Q.7 Find the value of \(x\), if \[ \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 4\\ 5 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = \begin{vmatrix} 2x & 4\\ 6 & x \end{vmatrix} \]

उत्तर -

\[ \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 4\\ 5 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = \begin{vmatrix} 2x & 4\\ 6 & x \end{vmatrix} \]

\[ (2)(1)-(4)(5) =(2x)(x)-(4)(6) \]

\[ 2-20 =2x^2-24 \]

\[ -18 =2x^2-24 \]

\[ 2x^2 =6 \]

\[ x^2 =3 \]

\[ x=\pm\sqrt3 \]

Q.7 (ii) Find the value of \(x\), if \[ \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 3\\ 4 & 5 \end{vmatrix} = \begin{vmatrix} x & 3\\ 2x & 5 \end{vmatrix} \]

उत्तर -

\[ \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 3\\ 4 & 5 \end{vmatrix} = \begin{vmatrix} x & 3\\ 2x & 5 \end{vmatrix} \]

\[ (2)(5)-(3)(4) =(x)(5)-(3)(2x) \]

\[ 10-12 =5x-6x \]

\[ -2 =-x \]

\[ x=2 \]

\[ \therefore x=2 \]

Q.8 If \[ \begin{vmatrix} x & 2\\ 18 & x \end{vmatrix} = \begin{vmatrix} 6 & 2\\ 18 & 6 \end{vmatrix} \] then \(x\) is equal to

(A) 6    (B) \(\pm6\)    (C) -6    (D) 0

उत्तर -

\[ \begin{vmatrix} x & 2\\ 18 & x \end{vmatrix} = \begin{vmatrix} 6 & 2\\ 18 & 6 \end{vmatrix} \]

\[ x^2-(2)(18) =(6)(6)-(2)(18) \]

\[ x^2-36 =36-36 \]

\[ x^2=36 \]

\[ x=\pm6 \]

\[ \therefore \boxed{x=\pm6} \]

अतः सही विकल्प (B) \(\pm6\) है।
